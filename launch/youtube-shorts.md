The Country That Deleted an Entire Day | Samoa 2011 #Shorts

## Description (ready to paste)

In 2011, Samoa did something extraordinary: it skipped Friday, December 30.

The country went straight from Thursday, December 29, to Saturday, December 31. Why? To bring its calendar into closer alignment with trading partners like Australia and New Zealand.

Even the missing day's wages were addressed by law. Employees whose normal working hours were lost were entitled to equivalent pay and allowances.

A country didn't change the laws of physics. It changed the calendar.
