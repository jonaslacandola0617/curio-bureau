## Title
The Town That's Been Burning Since 1962 | Centralia #Shorts

## Description

A town with a fire burning underneath it since 1962. 🔥

Centralia, Pennsylvania, sits above abandoned coal-mine workings where a fire has burned for decades. Roads were damaged. Most residents relocated. Yet the fire has never been fully extinguished.

In 1983, an official study estimated extinguishing the fire would cost $663 million. The next year, Congress allocated $42 million for property acquisitions and relocation.

How does an underground fire outlast almost an entire town?
