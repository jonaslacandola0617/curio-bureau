Imagine waking up and realizing Friday never happened. 📅

In 2011, Samoa skipped December 30 entirely. Thursday the 29th became Saturday the 31st—all to align its calendar with its biggest trading partners.

Even stranger? Workers who would normally have worked that Friday were still entitled to be paid.

Would you skip a Friday… or keep the extra 24 hours? 👀
