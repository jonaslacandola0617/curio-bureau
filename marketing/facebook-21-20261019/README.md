## Review notes before uploading

The catalog contains 21 complete movies, not 21 newly certified final masters. It preserves historical status.

- **Astronaut Height:** the owner reported a clipped final spoken word; check the archived ending before posting.
- **Black Dwarf V2, Mint V2, Onion Tears V1 and Phantom Vibration V1:** full exports exist; complete-film owner review remains pending.
- **Ghost Army:** this is the complete original EP009 R2 candidate. Later rejected short collage tests are excluded. R2 itself is not a current approved style or final-release benchmark.
- **Lake Peigneur:** selected the complete natural-voice revision, not the instrumental-only audio repair. Its historical acceptance was “good enough” for analysis.
- **Cold Metal:** the accepted complete film is 38.17 seconds at 720p. The 13.9-second 4K file is only a motion test.
- **Earlier episodes:** existing creative, listening, caption and item-specific audio-rights gaps remain documented. In particular, the Samoa stock-SFX manifest does not certify per-file licensing, and Mars/Ghost Army retain sample-library review items. See the notes alongside each draft and provenance links below.

No new editing, rendering, upscaling, publishing, blanket rights clearance or fresh whole-film approval is claimed. Current download checks are recorded in [LINK_CHECKS.json](LINK_CHECKS.json); hashes and recorded dimensions are in the catalog.
