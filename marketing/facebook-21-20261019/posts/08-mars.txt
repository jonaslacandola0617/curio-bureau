The spacecraft reached Mars. Its signal never came back.

In 1999, a mismatch between pound-force seconds and newton seconds in ground software helped send Mars Climate Orbiter onto the wrong approach.

The failure was bigger than a conversion: the checks that should have caught it failed, too.
