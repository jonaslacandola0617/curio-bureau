In 1969, engineers stopped the flow over Niagara’s American Falls. 🌊

They diverted the water so they could examine the rock beneath the waterfall and study the debris piled at its base. Horseshoe Falls kept flowing.

For a few months, a famous curtain of water became an exposed landscape.
