Sunlight looks white. So why do we picture a yellow Sun? ☀️

Visible sunlight blends many colors. The atmosphere changes the light that reaches us, and some scientific solar images assign colors to wavelengths our eyes cannot see.

A yellow drawing, a red sunset and a colorful solar image are telling different stories.
