A lake began draining into the ground—and the canal feeding the coast reversed direction.

In November 1980, water from Louisiana’s Lake Peigneur flooded the salt mine below. A huge whirlpool swallowed equipment and shoreline, leaving a permanently changed landscape.

The miners escaped. The lake was never the same.
