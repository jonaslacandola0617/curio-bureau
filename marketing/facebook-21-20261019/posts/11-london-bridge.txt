London Bridge is in Arizona—and the water beneath it came later. 🌵

After the old bridge was sold in 1968, its granite facing stones were shipped to Lake Havasu City and fitted around a new concrete structure.

Crews rebuilt it on dry land, then dug the channel underneath.
