Your fingers don’t wrinkle in water simply because the skin soaks it up.

The familiar folds involve an active nervous-system response and changes in the blood vessels beneath the skin.

Could the wrinkles help with wet grip? Some studies suggest benefits, but the evidence is mixed. The explanation is more interesting than the usual answer.
