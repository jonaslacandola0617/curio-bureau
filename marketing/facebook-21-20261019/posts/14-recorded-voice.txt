Why does your recorded voice sound like someone else? 🎙️

When you speak, you hear sound through the air and vibrations through your skull and other tissues. An ordinary microphone captures the airborne route, leaving out part of the experience you know.

The microphone and room affect playback, too. Both versions are you—heard differently.
