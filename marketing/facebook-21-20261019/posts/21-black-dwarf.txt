A dead star. A falling limit. One more possible ending.

A theoretical black-dwarf model explores how an extremely slow change in composition could make some massive stellar remnants collapse in the far future—if protons do not decay first.

This is a hypothesis, not an observed explosion or a prediction for our Sun.
