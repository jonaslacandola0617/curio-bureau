If the Sun disappeared, daylight wouldn’t end instantly. ☀️

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth. In this impossible thought experiment, even the change in Earth’s orbit would take time to arrive.

For those final minutes, we would still be living in the Sun’s past.

The Sun cannot actually vanish; this is a lesson about how information travels.
