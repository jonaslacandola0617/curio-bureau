## Scope and exclusions

TRPM8 participates in cold sensing; it is not the only biological sensor. No claim that menthol never changes tissue temperature, that every mint species has the same menthol content, or that a whole person becomes colder. The channel print is a labeled conceptual figure, not molecular imaging or a literal key-lock shape.
