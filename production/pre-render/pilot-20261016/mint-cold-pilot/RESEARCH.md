## Claim lock proposal

- Menthol and cooling can activate TRPM8. Support: M1
- Cooling sensation is not itself a thermometer reading. Support: Inference from M1; no whole-body temperature assertion.

## Required boundaries

TRPM8 is part of the system, not its only sensor. Do not assert menthol never changes any tissue temperature or has only one biological target. Receptor gate illustration is a labeled schematic, not observed molecular motion. No health, breathing or treatment claim.

All extra molecular, numerical, health or equipment claims require their own evidence before adoption. This is a draft source ledger, not an independent fact-check approval.
