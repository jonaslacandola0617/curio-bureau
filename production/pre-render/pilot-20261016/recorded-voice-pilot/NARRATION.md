# Why your recorded voice sounds unfamiliar — complete narration v2

Your phone records your voice. So why does it sound unfamiliar? When you speak, sound reaches your ears through the air. Vibrations through your head also contribute to what you hear. An ordinary microphone captures airborne sound, not that same internal contribution. Play it back, and the balance is different. That helps explain why a familiar voice can sound strange. But a recording is not a perfect copy of what everyone else hears either. The room, microphone and speaker can change it too. Same voice. Different routes to your ears.
