# Why your recorded voice sounds unfamiliar

DRAFT — not recorded or owner-approved. Timing follows complete Bernard speech, never fixed scene slots.

Your phone records your voice. So why does it sound like someone else? When you speak, sound reaches your ears through the air. Vibrations also travel through your head, contributing to what you hear. An ordinary microphone captures the airborne sound, not that same internal contribution. Play the recording back, and the balance is different. That helps explain why your familiar voice can suddenly sound unfamiliar. But a recording is not a perfect copy of what everyone else hears either. The microphone, room and speaker can change it too. Same voice. Different routes to your ears.
