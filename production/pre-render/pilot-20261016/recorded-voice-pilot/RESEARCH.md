## Claim lock proposal

- Air and body-conducted contributions differ from ordinary microphone playback. Support: V1,V2
- Do not claim the recording is precisely what others hear. Support: Production qualification; equipment/room remain confounds.

## Required boundaries

No universal bass boost, pitch shift or percentage. Any processed before/after audio is explicitly an illustration, not a reconstruction of every viewer’s self-voice. Recover the approved capture source before reuse; old five-state beatboard is rejected.

All extra molecular, numerical, health or equipment claims require their own evidence before adoption. This is a draft source ledger, not an independent fact-check approval.
