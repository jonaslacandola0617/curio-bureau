# Why metal feels colder than wood

DRAFT — not recorded or owner-approved. Timing follows complete Bernard speech, never fixed scene slots.

These can be the same temperature. So why does metal feel colder? Imagine a metal spoon and a wooden spoon left in the same room until they settle to the room’s temperature. Your hand is warmer. Touch them, and heat flows out of your skin. The metal carries that energy away faster, so the skin touching it cools more quickly. Wood transfers it more slowly. The colder feeling is not proof that the metal started colder. Your sense of temperature is responding to heat transfer, not simply reading a thermometer. Same room. Same starting temperature. A very different feeling.
