## Why the Sun is not simply yellow
A familiar visual misconception, explained with cleared scientific photographs and physical-paper evidence. Sunlight is white; atmospheric scattering changes its appearance, especially near the horizon. Camera filters and false-color scientific images require explicit identification and cannot serve as proof of visible color. Do not infer intrinsic color from an orange UV image or an overexposed photograph.
