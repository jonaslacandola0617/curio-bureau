- Thick Playfair Display 900 titles; Manrope utility/captions; owner-approved bright selective emphasis and material treatment. Preserve reading holds and source-local annotations. Captions stay screen-fixed around 42–58% height.
- Voice A/Bernard is the default; repeat auditions only for a specific reason. Real speech timing governs final motion. Do not label machine-reconciled phrases independently word-verified. Unexpected >0.85s speech gaps require editorial correction; >1.2s between passages is a hard release failure.
- Inventory existing sounds first. Generate only for a real gap, then save/catalog/push the new asset. Do not promise perceptual quality from waveform measurements. Music, narration and physically/editorially meaningful sound cues require listening review.
- Sources, maps, quantities and asset rights must be verified; reconstructions/schematics/transcriptions are disclosed. No copied tutorial pixels/audio or raw restricted materials in public code.
- Native 2160×3840, 30fps MP4 and WebM remain required. Preserve synchronized full-length browser export. Use complete 1080p creative review before expensive native production.
- Fonts, captions/UI, speech gaps, full encoded decode, duration, delivered loudness (-14 to -12 LUFS, ≤-1 dBTP), rights and human creative/listening approval remain independent release gates. Automated engine checks cover only their stated scope.
- R2 is the positive moving benchmark. Rejected Ghost Army V1–V4 are not templates. Shared components do not prescribe repeated layouts, maps or choreography.
