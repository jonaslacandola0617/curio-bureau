## 1. Images carry the story
Use specific, cleared photographs, photographic cutouts, scientific/medical images or photographs of physical models as the primary visual actors. The viewer sees the narrated observation, comparison, mechanism and consequence. A caption or decorative photograph cannot substitute for visual explanation. Establish source identity, then inspect, isolate, compare or animate the relevant feature. Choose maximum crop and resolution before asset production. Label model photographs, instrument images, composites and illustrative transformations honestly. Never fabricate an archival source, clinical measurement or documentary event.

Paper is a supporting material: image print, clipping edge, backing, source excerpt, small factual strip or evidence document where warranted. It is not the mandatory central subject or a full-page replacement for a usable image. Replace page/highlight-only beats with a photographic action or comparison when that explains better. Avoid drawn assets as the default escape from difficult imagery. Necessary charts/maps/diagrams remain refined source-attached or printed explanatory support; procedural construction is judged by resulting craft, precision and purpose. No freestanding generic vector environment, anatomical placeholder or rectangle masquerading as a specific object.

## 2. Directed, visibly meaningful motion
Plan factual argument → connected shots and initial/intermediate/resolved states → choreography → image/layer requirements → finished art → actual moving review. Preserve continuity of visual thought. Known image → purposeful operation → visibly changed understanding.

Use decisive camera approaches and close-ups; let an important image reach/cross the viewport edges when native source detail permits. Reveal real details instead of timid whole-frame zooms. Prepare clean semantic cutouts and credible hidden regions. Where depth serves the beat, foreground, middle and background move independently at noticeably different rates; opposing travel, separate scale and coherent foreground occlusion make parallax legible. A flat image zoom is not parallax. Establish the subject before the closest crop; protect crucial faces/features. Full exits and deliberate intermediate composition are mandatory.

Creatively stack unequal images with different scale, angle, overlap and depth according to narrative hierarchy. Let a later source already occupy the world behind the active one when useful. Avoid repeated centered postcard/card layouts, poster-by-poster delivery, rigid isolated object holds or drift alone filling a narrated beat. Animate the photographed object or compare its states where truthful. Mark exaggerated or conceptual deformation; do not suggest bones grew, an image is a real measurement, or an astronaut was clinically measured in a context photograph.

Keep recognition, inference and reading holds. Motion need not be perpetual or random; every primary move explains, selects, reveals, compares or hands off a thought. Use asymmetric arrival/settling, cutting at actual registered peak velocity where appropriate, camera-followed source-local highlights, meaningful cuts and object-led transitions. Headlines animate; screen-fixed captions do not inherit scene camera movement.

## 3. Strong, intentional material and optical finish
Maintain visible paper fiber, print/plastic surface, photocopy density and ink/material treatment by object role. Texture must visibly read at phone size. Creatively layer complementary textures, not indiscriminately pile every texture on every image. Preserve evidence detail and scientific color meaning.

Use compound/selective blur with coordinated gradient darkness/brightness to focus an image, map or source detail. Short focus hunts settle sharply. Retain restrained chromatic separation, film grain, mechanical image jitter, slight brightness/exposure variation, and source-local ink bleed/edge boil where suited to printed lines. Local ink may sample at ~12fps while delivery remains 30fps. Source highlights/annotations stay registered as the camera follows their motion. No effect hides a broken mask, full-exit defect or weak art. The locked ending gets no additional texture/effects.

## 4. Sound is forward and synchronized
Reuse approved Bernard. Speech timing drives motion; no routine repeated voice auditions or unnecessary rerecording. Preserve expressive original evolving music and meaningful audible action Foley. Short shutter, image/paper handling, marker, zoom, reveal and transition events need clearly audible matching sound; incidental drift need not trigger constant effects.

Owner requested action SFX around 80–90% of music/narration rather than buried effects. Treat this as a perceptual direction: compare short cue windows with typical voiced/music windows, adjust for source crest factor and meaning, use selective music ducking and adequate codec headroom. Do not apply one global percentage to every cue or continuous bed. R2's recorded cue sheet and owner-approved full mix are the sound benchmark; waveform ratios alone are not listening certification. Check actual encoded AAC/Opus for audibility, intelligibility and peaks. Preserve an approved mix on resolution-only exports.

Inventory existing original/generated and all 70 owner-retained free editorial cues FIRST. Generate only for a genuine gap. Any generated effect is downloaded, catalogued and pushed for reuse where rights permit. Raw restricted provider stock and isolated stock stems remain private, with hash-verified retrieval metadata and licenses in the repo. No repeated effect generation for each film.
