## 10. Paper recipe: approved implementation, not a universal law

`paper_sheet(w,h)` creates a cached static neutral zero-mean field, seeded20261021. Coarse formation is generated at roughly h/24×w/24 and bicubically enlarged. Full-resolution tooth is separate. About w*h/650 fibers are drawn with lengths7–53px, varied near-horizontal slopes, one-pixel widths with occasional two-pixel fibers, shades91/103/151/164 on a128 baseline. A0.42px blur integrates fibers.

Combine formation×3.2 + tooth×2.1 + centered fiber field×0.48, then remove the mean. Multiply assembled normalizedRGB by `1 + paper * .0042`, clip safely. Paper remains static while film grain moves; it reads especially on light paper/source surfaces without cloudy tint. This is neutral designed material, not an authentic scanned document or new evidence.

For new projects retain separate paper/material controls and tune to native asset scale and encoded appearance. Use source-specific rock, skin, liquid, print or photographic detail where appropriate. The approved recipe is a reliable starting point; it is not a claim that all scientific surfaces should look like identical paper or that numeric identity guarantees equivalent artistry.

## 11. Foreground film-grain recipe

The grain bank ports an established Cold Metal compositing approach. Each deterministic bank uses seed `9182 + k*7717`. Two uniform512×512 fields combine into a triangular noise distribution centered128, amplitude115. Resize to1024×1024 with nearest-neighbor sampling. Sixteen banks are cached.

At authored time t, compute `tick=floor((t+1e-8)*12)`. Select bank tick mod16. Scroll sample offsets by83px vertically and137px horizontally per tick, modulo1024, so that an obvious stationary tile does not sit behind the artwork. Grain covers the whole composition, including actual photograph/map rectangles, not only empty borders.

Blend with standard soft-light behavior: for source s≤0.5, `a-(1-2*s)*a*(1-a)`; otherwise `a+(2*s-1)*(D(a)-a)`, where D is the low-luma cubic below a=.25 and square-root above it. The approved result is66% original +34% soft-light mix. Clamp/round to8-bitRGB only at output. Paper is applied before grain; captions remain above both.

This amount was approved in this film. It is not recovered proprietary Vox opacity. Native proof must show both spatial coverage and temporal change with source/exposure held constant. A native effect that disappears after H264 is unfinished; inspect actual encoded phone-size frames and motion.
