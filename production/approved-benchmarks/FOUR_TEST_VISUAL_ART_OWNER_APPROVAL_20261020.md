## What is adopted

1. **An image owns the explanation.** Choose a specific photographic subject, component or source. Its material, parts, geography or spatial relationships visibly perform the narrated change. Identify what the viewer understands before and after.
2. **Construct for the operation.** Plan initial, difficult intermediate and resolved states before polished assets. Separate movable components, semantic masks, occluders and repaired hidden regions as the mechanism requires. Maintain hinges, contacts, origins, source anchors and identity through the action.
3. **Compose each relationship deliberately.** Choose scale, crop, overlap, eye path and negative space around the exact mechanism and centered caption footprint. A clean source-to-model inspection cut is valid. Every topic gets its own visual solution; the four test layouts are examples, not four reusable page templates.
4. **Carry meaning between states.** Preserve the relevant object, geographic frame, comparison baseline or question. Build cause, transfer and consequence in order; allow a resolved reading hold. Camera movement reveals a relationship or detail.
5. **Give scientific explanation a physical/source context.** Refined vector components, fluid masks and diagrams can support the photographed subject or declared conceptual model. The approved brine comparison and claw rig establish that a useful diagram need not be forced onto a paper sheet. A generated model stays distinguishable from documentary or measured evidence.
6. **Keep labels clear through the entire action.** Source-attached labels follow their sources; date/category changes remain readable at the event, under captions and platform controls. The final Diomede/Brine v2b corrections are part of the selected benchmark.
7. **Maintain the same art quality through the difficult middle and payoff.** Specific multi-part art and purposeful intermediate states are the floor for all scenes, not only the opener. Method remains flexible: judge the result, its clarity and scientific truth.

## Final-touch requirement for future production

Material texture and overlays must be visibly stronger than these art studies at ordinary phone size **after encoding**. Author the treatment for each surface: mineral/rock detail, photographic grain, print/plastic/fiber where the source calls for them, fluid/surface detail and locally registered overlays. Preserve source identity, scientific color distinctions and sharp evidence. A global low-opacity noise layer alone does not establish a finished material stack.

Retain brief, motivated **focus hunts that settle sharp**, selected-source focus with coherent luminance, appropriate camera/transition blur, restrained image chromatic separation, mechanical jitter/exposure variation and local ink bleed/edge boil on printed marks where applicable. Record actual windows and exclusions individually. The earlier test look-back is a scope-limited historical report, not blanket permission to omit the usual techniques in final videos. Do not apply every effect everywhere; choose and demonstrate each appropriate treatment.

Keep Playfair900, Manrope and graphics crisp above image optics; no blurred text shadows, halos or haze. Preserve the exact shared30fps ending after finishing. Before native capture inspect material and focus A/B evidence; after encoding inspect phone-size visibility, sharp holds, key label changes and the ending. Follow the existing [finishing contract](../engine/FINISHING_OUTPUT_CONTRACT.md) and [finishing checklist](../engine/FINISHING_CHECKLIST.md).

## Regression gate for every new visual

Use [VISUAL_ART_REVIEW.example.json](../templates/sun-standard/VISUAL_ART_REVIEW.example.json) with the existing shot/sequence contracts. For each beat, name the proposition, visual operation, invariant, image/component plan, difficult state, caption clearance, next-state inheritance and finishing evidence. Compare demanding actual frames and the hardest join to the approved examples. Review without narration/headings while retaining necessary factual labels, then with real captions. Check the key event itself, not only nearby frames.

Reject a sequence whose apparent progress comes only from headings, unrelated image entrances, drifting whole photographs, random animation or added noise. Reject an unprepared flat asset that cannot perform the action, a disconnected annotation, an unexplained quality drop or the same page layout repeated with new pictures. Revise the composition/assets/operation before adding more effects. A declaration, JSON pass or valid codec cannot approve artistic quality.
