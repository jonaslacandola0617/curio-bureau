## Proposed only / deliberately NOT adopted
Exact Bézier curves or After Effects stack/plug-ins, source project files, physical golf aerodynamics solver fidelity, automatic archival-image extraction/rehosting, automated historical map topology, speculative “Vox-style” layout clones, numeric viewer-retention effects, source film photo-cards, news footage as animation, and frame-perfect cut boundaries for every five-second broad survey interval.

### Preserved
Playfair Display and Manrope, Curio palette and brand, original recognisable multi-part illustration, centered 42–58% captions, meaningful real VO/SFX actions, 2160×3840 true native 30fps two independently encoded MP4/WebM masters, no long speech gaps, fact and rights standards. ### Limit
Eight original moving demonstrations each **540×960 30fps/102 frames/3.4 sec**, technically validated; not a 4K film; no expert perceptual comparison/retention proof. Five source films comprehensively **coarse scanned**, but 96 intervals and 25 R/P skips are not frame-exact shot segmentation. Human impression quality still requires independent review for full film approval.
