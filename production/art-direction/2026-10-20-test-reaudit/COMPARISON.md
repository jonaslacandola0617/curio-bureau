# Four tests rejected: a second Vox comparison

## Finding and correction of the earlier judgment

All four 12-second tests fail to demonstrate the requested improvement in visual art and animation. The earlier review's conclusion that they met the Vox learning goals was too generous. It correctly identified some connected explanatory states, but treated those states as sufficient evidence of accomplished art direction. They are not sufficient. A line attached to a photograph can satisfy an operation contract while the finished result still looks like a slide with annotations.

The owner's rejection supersedes the internal creative acceptance in `REVIEW_NOTES.md`. Technical delivery and file-integrity results remain valid. No owner approval existed. Preserve the films as rejected experiments, rather than changing their bytes or relabeling them as benchmarks.

The recurring construction is visible across the actual encoded films: an isolated image establishes the noun; a headline introduces the claim; captions occupy the middle; a few simple graphic elements enact the explanation; a concluding label announces the answer. There are four subjects, but much less variety in the visual language, asset construction and dramatic action. The source photograph often remains inert even when the mechanism requires something within it to change.

## What the stronger source sequences actually do

### A. Build the action into the asset

In Watwani's cotton-picking excerpt (`answered-14`, 0–1.16s), the hand is already inside the cotton field's visual space. Between sampled states the fingers change pose, the cotton separates from the plant and the hand carries it. Foreground arm, grasp, cotton and background have distinct roles. The motion communicates a physical action; a pointing ring would not substitute for it.

The adjacent flag-to-cotton excerpt (`answered-12`, 0–1.12s) adds plants at different scales and overlapping depths around an established flag. The scene gains a foreground and a visual environment. This is more specific than placing a subject cutout at the bottom of a blank branded field. Its lesson is staging and asset design, not a requirement that every science video contain a detailed landscape.

Our shrimp has an open claw in the first state and the same open claw in the inspection. The jet begins later without an initiating anatomical action. That omission is the center of the scene, not a minor polish limitation. Choosing a flattened specimen whose claw cannot articulate constrained the sequence before animation began.

### B. Simple graphics can still perform the mechanism

The fan excerpt (`answered-13`, 0–1.28s) is intentionally simple. Fan blades rotate; airflow strokes originate beside the working machine, extend and curl outward, then clear. The explanatory subject itself acts. A gray background and a small object are not inherently failures.

The drought excerpt (`answered-17`, 0–2.36s) coordinates changes in leaves, water imagery, terrain and palette inside the same illustration. Around the middle samples the foliage and ground begin to lose their earlier condition; the later state has a visibly drier environment. Several related properties change together. The unchanged “Dry conditions” label cannot account for that visual difference by itself.

These are useful controls against the wrong remedy. Our problem is not solved by banning circles, demanding photorealism everywhere or filling every empty pixel. A restrained diagram succeeds when its depicted parts and relationships are sufficient to make the change clear. The smooth bowl and descending disk in our brine test communicate a generic sinking event, but leave most of the distinctive underwater-lake explanation outside the artwork.

### C. Change representation while preserving the unit being discussed

Mariano's district sequence (`parties-1`, 0–8.10s) moves from geographic districts to vote-share rings and then representative figures, keeping a consistent array. Around 2–3s rings take over the districts; later figures occupy corresponding positions. Shape, distribution and representation are different stages of the same argument. The geography is doing more than supplying a backdrop for words.

Another sequence (`parties-4`, 0–4.50s) gathers smaller districts into larger state forms, then brings people into those groups. The layout reorganizes to explain a changed grouping. These passages use simple shapes and photographic cutouts effectively because the relationships between them are authored and readable.

Our Diomede marker and calendar do share timing, which is a real explanatory connection. But the satellite photograph stays a largely unchanged rectangle while a separate card supplies the date convention. Temporal synchronization alone has not made them one convincing geographic composition.

### D. Construct a visual event from cooperating materials

The epigenetics liquid breakdown (`epigenetics-0`, 0–about 2.4s) explicitly identifies separate ingredients: a drop image, drop footage, water imagery, repeated shapes, a splash image and a dish image. The later portion places the combined event into a designed scientific workspace. This is direct process evidence of constituent assets, rather than a guess about software layers.

The important difference is the construction of a liquid event from suitable parts. It does not establish that more layers are always better, or that we should copy its yellow palette and scrapbook arrangement. Our cyan line on concrete has the color convention “water” but little of the surface, edge, transparency or interaction that could make the change convincing within the chosen photographic treatment.

### E. An inspection changes the working composition

In the DNA export (`epigenetics-1`, 0–6.50s), the first portion shows a construction view of the helix and markers; later states place the model inside a textured scientific image and a magnifier selects marked material. The finished context has photographic edges, overlapping objects and a clear inspection hierarchy. Process-to-finish changes in this export are not assumed to be episode transitions.

In `parties-10` (0–4.70s), a magnifier crosses a chart and reveals an election-rule composition. Later states resolve to that rule and its representative figure. The lens introduces the next question and gives the following view a reason to occupy the frame. It is not simply a circular crop around the same picture.

Our shrimp inspection cross-dissolves two differently positioned views while both briefly remain visible. The circle identifies a detail, but the view inside it does not acquire the moving anatomical information needed to explain the snap. A circle is an outline around an unresolved asset problem.

## Direct comparison of each delivered test

Times below are navigation ranges in the 12-second Curio exports. The denser sheets support sampled states within those ranges, not exact edit-frame declarations.

### 01 — Roman concrete

**Observed:** At 0–2s a macro plate and large lower headline establish a crack/repair premise. The crack draws through the plate as a thick, angular dark stroke. At roughly 2.1–3s the plate enlarges. During 3.5–6.8s cyan strokes and small light dots supply water/transfer notation. Across the sampled 6.8–8.3s join the seam becomes pale, while the adjacent aggregate and lime clast remain essentially the same image. The closing line announces that the gap can seal.

**Specific construction problem:** The renderer draws a constant-width polyline over the intact material image, then paints a textured stroke over that path. It does not reveal fractured material faces or an opening with depth. The repair is an overlay replacement. The chemistry is represented by moving dots without a convincing visible change in the selected material. The plate is attractive, but the working assets are not adequate for the selected photographic mechanism.

**Vox comparison:** The epigenetics liquid breakdown shows that an event can require distinct edge, liquid, particulate and contact assets. The drought illustration demonstrates coordinated change in the affected subject. Our test changes a path's appearance while leaving most of the subject untouched. It has causal notation, but weak material behavior.

**Verdict:** Failure in material construction and transformation, despite a readable open-to-filled distinction. Calling it “image-owned” was an inadequate reason to accept its craft.

**Required redesign, if later authorized:** Design the fracture, exposed clast and repair states as cooperating artwork. Decide whether this is a photographic inspection or an explicitly printed scientific model; then give water entry, material release and deposition a consistent visual treatment. A material event can use simplified scientific conventions without pretending to be measured microscopy. The repair must develop within the opening rather than conceal an intact image with a replacement stroke. Do not add unsupported chemical detail simply to look sophisticated.

### 02 — Diomede / International Date Line

**Observed:** The initial image straightens, island names appear, a dashed boundary grows, and the headline leaves. The photograph then rests in the same upper-middle region. A lower card appears. Across 5.6–7.1s the marker crosses the line while Monday scrolls to Tuesday; later example-day labels appear on the islands.

**Specific construction problem:** The viewer has to alternate between a tiny marker on the photograph and a large detached calendar. Much of the vertical field is unused by the explanation. The satellite image provides credible location, but the composition does not develop geography into the date convention: the moving marker and a word swap do nearly all the work. The top headline, center narration and bottom conclusion also repeat interpretations rather than creating a stronger spatial argument.

**Vox comparison:** The district-to-ring-to-representative sequence makes the same units acquire new explanatory forms, preserving their layout. Our map and date stay in separate containers. A calendar card is not intrinsically wrong; it becomes weak when it dominates the answer without strengthening the spatial relationship.

**Verdict:** Failure in composition and integration. The crossing is logically coordinated, but the artwork still reads as an illustrated slide.

**Required redesign, if later authorized:** Let the two geographic sides carry the example-day distinction and make the crossing the dominant event. Establish enough orientation and scale for the islands to be places rather than two anonymous shapes; avoid spending a short passage on an unnecessary global tour. Recompose around the central captions rather than reserving an empty field. Maintain explicit illustrative-day conventions; neither “always different dates” nor an invariant 24-hour time difference is justified.

### 03 — Pistol shrimp

**Observed:** At 0–3s the whole specimen rests low in a large navy field. Around 3.3–4.3s the whole shrimp fades while a relocated claw crop appears. The encoded 3.7s and 4.0s join samples show both views overlapping. At about 5–8s thin cyan strokes extend into clean nested ellipses. The ellipses collapse and expanding rings/“SNAP” give the consequence. The selected claw remains open.

**Specific construction problem:** The main actor never performs its causal action. The crop enlarges the specimen but adds no mechanism. The jet is a set of long straight strokes disconnected from convincing water behavior; the bubble is a clean outline. The cutout, diagram and emphatic word have different levels of representation without a strong transition between them. The empty field makes the asset limitation particularly exposed.

**Vox comparison:** The cotton-picking hand changes pose and transfers matter; the fan's blades move before airflow leaves the machine. Those examples establish action in the actual working subject, even with simple illustration. The DNA inspection adds an internal working relationship rather than merely enlarging the original noun. Our specimen cannot support the planned event.

**Verdict:** The clearest failure of mechanism animation. A disclaimer that the claw does not articulate described the central defect; it did not excuse accepting it.

**Required redesign, if later authorized:** Obtain or build scientifically supportable separate claw components and an inspection view that can actually perform the initiating movement. Choose a readable, explicitly slowed conceptual jet/cavity treatment, with the collapse and pulse located consistently. A source-attached printed figure may support the explanation. A changing outline cannot stand in for every phase of the event. Review anatomy and spatial relationships before decorating them with specimen texture.

### 04 — Underwater brine pool

**Observed:** A real photograph establishes the anomalous shoreline, then an approximate yellow trace selects it. Between about 4.4–5.2s the photo moves into a shallow upper strip. A separate smooth beige basin appears below. A dotted yellow disk descends, fades/spreads and becomes a level-topped yellow pool. Labels identify seawater and brine.

**Specific construction problem:** The image that makes the subject unusual is demoted just when the causal explanation begins. The generic basin does not carry recognizable features of the selected shoreline, nor does the transition establish a specific section plane. Because it is honestly labeled conceptual, it should not be condemned as a falsely measured section; the failure is its weak visual relationship and generic art. The disk and dots show sinking, but do little to explain extra salt producing a density difference or two fluids occupying the same depression.

**Vox comparison:** The drought illustration keeps the environmental relationships legible as several properties change. The district examples preserve identifiable units when changing representation. Our photo and bowl are related by subject and vertical stacking, with little feature continuity. The liquid-composite process also offers a more suitable material vocabulary than a filled disk, if a photographic treatment is selected.

**Verdict:** Failure at the photo-to-model handoff and in the specificity of the mechanism artwork. A flat pool surface is a worthwhile physical correction, not proof of strong art direction.

**Required redesign, if later authorized:** Choose between a deliberately source-attached conceptual section and a sufficiently designed photographic inspection; establish the relationship explicitly. Preserve recognizable basin/shoreline information without inventing measured geometry. Make the density comparison readable through equal-volume conventions or another accurate comparison before showing collection. Do not silently imply that a perfectly isolated ball of brine falls through the sea in the observed event.

| A photograph hands off to a conceptual section with a level surface. | The specific setting gives way to a generic bowl and disk. | A correct convention does not establish a convincing visual handoff. |
| Texture, overlays, focus and captions were implemented. | The same sparse layout and limited asset behavior remain visible. | Finishing cannot compensate for undeveloped artwork. |

The earlier documents already required custom artwork, continuity and creative review. This was an execution and judgment failure, not a missing rule that another checklist will solve. The renderer's four scene functions also show the recurring convenience: one photograph/cutout per topic, a navy/ivory field, shared label/line/ring helpers, fixed caption construction and small parametric operations. Canvas can render much richer scenes; the failure was what was designed and supplied to it.

The internal review softened central omissions into “remaining limits.” That language was especially wrong for the shrimp: failure to animate the initiating action should have changed the disposition, not become a footnote beneath acceptance. Similarly, conceptual chemistry was used to excuse weak material rendering. Scientific simplification and visual underdevelopment are different judgments.

## What a credible correction would have to demonstrate

This is an analysis deliverable, not authorization for another render. Future work should demonstrate improvement in the visible work itself:

1. **A working subject:** assets contain the parts and intermediate states required by the mechanism. A beautiful flattened noun is insufficient when the action requires articulation, an interior or changing material.
2. **A composed explanation:** the dominant object, useful negative space and text placement are chosen around the actual relationship. The centered caption standard remains; an empty permanent caption floor is not required by it.
3. **A designed intermediate:** the difficult middle has authored geometry, occlusion, contact and readable state. Smooth easing cannot rescue an incoherent overlap or a missing action.
4. **Compatible materials:** texture, photographic edges, transparency, lighting and schematic marks establish a consistent representational convention. Grain pasted over a sparse scene is not equivalent to designed material detail.
5. **An earned consequence:** the visible subject action creates the resolved state. A closing word does not certify that the viewer saw the mechanism.

These criteria are proposals for judging implementation of existing requirements, not newly adopted channel policy. Do not demand visual complexity for its own sake: the fan excerpt is a useful counterexample. Do not copy Vox branding, shot layouts or proprietary assets. The goal is comparable specificity and explanatory craft in original Curio artwork.
