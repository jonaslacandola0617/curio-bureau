## Stronger default production brief

Science stories use recognizable real subject imagery, evidence inspection and refined printed mechanisms inside one coherent paper/image world. The camera discovers meaningful details. Bright source-local annotation directs attention. Visible fibers, print/plastic treatment and local ink behavior establish material identity. Parallax needs genuine separated layers and repaired hidden regions. Focus resolves sharply; informative holds remain valid. Composition and asset quality are judged before extensive finishing.

Five demanding frames now include a difficult intermediate state. Every applicable art dimension receives PASS/REVISE/NOT REVIEWED with frame/time and correction; the hardest join requires moving phone-scale review. Declaration checks remain distinct from subjective approval. No new arbitrary “Vox fidelity percentage” or complexity quota introduced.

Preserved: owner-approved films, browser/render/audio implementation, approved material recipe strengths, exact endcard pixels/curve, Bernard default, existing-SFX-first policy, original score standard, truthful scientific sourcing and native two-format release gates.
