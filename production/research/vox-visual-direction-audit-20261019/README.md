# Vox and Curio: visual direction audit

19 October 2026. Research and diagnosis only. Relativistic Dust is paused for this review. No new film, artwork, voice, soundtrack or animation test was produced. The eight Dust art states remain unapproved candidates.

**Finding:** Curio has developed a recognizable finish, but its compositions too often make the photograph identify a noun while headlines, captions and a small secondary figure explain the idea. The strongest Vox passages make the dominant visual relationship carry the reasoning. Closing that gap requires changing the construction of the sequence before making its assets, rather than increasing texture, zooms or animation frequency.

Read [the full comparative analysis](ANALYSIS.md), [the film-by-film scene atlas](SCENE_ATLAS.md), and [the passage evidence ledger](PASSAGES.md). [CORPUS.json](CORPUS.json) identifies the exact eleven source files and hashes; [DENSE_SAMPLES.json](DENSE_SAMPLES.json) records the decoded frame indices inspected. Source movies and screenshots from Vox are kept private and are not included here.

Evidence scope: five original uploaded Vox videos, approximately 37m55s of source, and six complete Curio exports, approximately 4m32s. Systematic sampled-frame survey across every film; 18 closer passages comprising 384 additional frames, spaced about half a second apart. This is not a claim of uninterrupted normal-speed viewing, listening QA, exhaustive shot segmentation, recovered proprietary rigs or mastery of all Vox work. Exact scope and timing limitations are in the analysis.

The adopted Sun production standard remains in force. Recommendations here are proposed creative corrections, not an approved channel redesign or a replacement style bible. Existing approvals and historical movie bytes remain intact. Kurzgesagt research is a separate, still-open obligation; this Vox audit does not complete it.
