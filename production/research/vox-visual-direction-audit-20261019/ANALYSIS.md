# Why the videos still feel assembled, and what the Vox comparison changes

## Judgment

The owner's criticism is substantially supported. Across six recent Curio exports, the recurring issue is not an absence of assets, motion, texture or conscientious science labels. It is a division of labor: a large attractive subject image supplies identity; a headline announces an interpretation; central subtitles narrate it; a separate smaller figure supplies the actual causal explanation. These elements can all be individually polished while their relationship remains weak.

Vox's stronger sequences distribute attention differently. A photograph becomes a measured comparison; a documented feature becomes a diagram element; an established map changes one boundary; a chart returns to reject a plausible explanation. The viewer learns through the changing relationship. The edit preserves something already understood while introducing the next uncertainty.

We are closer in material finish and basic annotation than in visual argument, hierarchy and continuity between representations. Sun and parts of Onion already demonstrate the better approach. This is a qualitative diagnosis of specific passages, not a numerical distance, a retention prediction or a claim that every Vox scene is superior.

## Evidence and limits

The corpus is five original Vox-titled uploads: Wildlife Trade, Electoral College, Golf Ball, Israel–Palestine and Fahrenheit. Their file hashes match the previously recorded source corpus. These are bounded examples from an older period of Vox, not a representative census of current output. Official source links and exact Curio versions are in CORPUS.json.

Every film was surveyed from beginning to end through contact sheets: 569 Vox frames at four-second sampling and 136 Curio frames at two-second sampling. Then 384 frame-index-selected images were inspected in 18 passages: 12 Vox windows of 12 seconds and six Curio windows of eight seconds. Total distinct inspected frame samples is 1,089, although some may depict duplicate held states. Dense sample times are derived from frame index and declared constant frame rate; their approximately half-second spacing cannot establish sub-frame timing, exact cut frames, easing curves or all transient overlaps.

The initial overview sheets label sampling bins by their starts. FFmpeg's fps filter selects a representative frame near the bin center: those labels are approximate navigation cues, with roughly two seconds of uncertainty for Vox and one for Curio. Only the dense ledger supplies actual selected-frame timestamps. Approximate atlas ranges must not be presented as exact shot boundaries. The study did not include complete normal-speed audiovisual playback or a new sound audit. Conclusions about precise rhythm, sound synchronization and continuous smoothness remain open. No proprietary After Effects project or audience analytics was available.

The analysis concerns visual form. It does not endorse dated pandemic explanations or certify historical/political claims in the reference films. In particular, early-2020 pathogen-route graphics should not be repurposed as established current medical evidence.

The earlier repository study deliberately excluded photographed imagery and recorded camera motion. The owner's present request explicitly includes images and scene composition, so this audit examines them. Recorded footage is distinguished from authored motion. An inferred layer/mask construction is never treated as an observed proprietary implementation.

## How the compositions work

### 1. The visual's job determines its size and placement

In Golf, 56–68s, the golfer cutout first identifies a person. A calibrated vertical distance strip appears behind him. Another historical cutout joins the same measuring space. The measurements become the organizing geometry; by about 67s, a broader chart occupies the field. The photograph is not indefinitely the hero simply because it is attractive. Its explanatory job finishes and the chart takes priority.

Our Black Dwarf passage at 30–38s does the reverse. The dark remnant remains a prominent, largely unchanged sphere. The important distinction—nearly fixed mass versus falling support limit—occurs in the smaller paper figure below it. The diagram is meaningful and scientifically more careful than a generic explosion, but the composition assigns less attention to the thing the viewer needs to understand.

The remedy is not to remove photographs. It is to let a source-attached figure, crop or internal inspection become dominant when that is where the claim lives. A photograph can establish identity, evidence or physical location; its role need not remain constant through every sentence.

### 2. Objects enter because a relationship needs them

Wildlife, 184–196s: a menu source fills the frame. Yellow circles identify particular animal illustrations. A dissolve then replaces that source with grouped animal nodes in a clean model. We see selection from evidence before abstraction. These are not simply two consecutive pictures of animals.

Fahrenheit, 100–112s: historical instruments and a portrait give way to a close view of a physical bulb. An annotation singles out its shape. A simplified thermal column follows. The photographic instrument and the simplified model are connected by the feature being explained; they are not interchangeable stock illustrations.

For Curio, the object list should therefore follow a reasoning requirement: what must be inspected, compared, opened or connected? Acquiring a mint leaf, ice cube, mouth photograph and receptor card independently creates a risk that the final edit will merely arrange four nouns. The receptor interaction must be planned first; the leaf and ice need entrances that lead attention into it.

### 3. A persistent reference frame makes change intelligible

Electoral, 92–104s and 112–124s, keeps Texas and Vermont in stable left/right positions. Counts and unit marks change representation. Population-to-elector relationships then become differently sized comparison blocks. Labels retain identity while the geometry changes the question being answered. This is intentional abstraction; simple squares are effective because their quantity and area carry information.

Israel–Palestine, 236–248s, keeps a regional map beside a named historical photograph. The photo moves through a shared workspace, arrows connect it to the geography, and the highlighted map area changes. There is considerable compositing, but the useful operation is the change in territory within a recognizable map. Copying wood grain or tilted prints would copy its surface, not its explanation.

Curio's strongest equivalent is Sun, 18–26s: the source disappears, the last-light boundary progresses along an established path, reaches Earth, and the city then becomes dark. Source, path and destination remain legible. The destination change is earned by the preceding state. This is the craft principle to preserve from the approved benchmark.

### 4. Text has distinct jobs

In these Vox references text may name an object, mark a measurement, identify provenance, select a phrase in a real document, pose a chapter question or carry a deliberate typographic argument. Those jobs are not all displayed at equal weight at once. In the Fahrenheit calibration passage, numbers sit beside positions on the thermal column; words identify those positions. Typography participates in the model.

Wildlife, 260–272s, establishes a whole document, moves to its title, then selects a specific clause. The highlight is evidence selection. It would lose that role if it were a generic yellow bar under a paraphrased headline on an invented page.

Curio frequently retains a large title, central caption, colored chip, diagram heading, object labels and small qualification simultaneously. Each has a defensible purpose in isolation. Together they make the viewer choose among several versions of the same explanation. Mint's two routes are genuinely connected, yet the receptor remains below the leaf and ice, with repeated headings explaining the relationship again.

Keep centered Manrope captions and Playfair 900. Reduce redundant editorial copy and let useful local labels do their job. A qualification may be necessary without becoming a second headline. Legibility and epistemic caution must survive the edit; shrinking all disclaimers is not a solution.

### 5. Transitions preserve a question or change its scale

Golf's 148–160s passage is particularly instructive. Airflow around a dimpled ball is compared with a smooth-ball wake. The earlier driving-distance chart then returns. A ball and historical pointer sit beside it, while a selected portion of the chart is highlighted. The sequence distinguishes a real mechanism from an explanation of a later change. It uses visual structure to test an answer, rather than collecting interesting facts about the object.

At 328–340s, the opening golf-course model returns. Its tee/trajectory relationship changes and the obstacle is marked. This revisits the initial stakes with a newly understood consequence. A repeated asset becomes a payoff because its state and meaning change.

Our repeated opening images too often return with a different headline but much the same state. Reuse itself is not the fault. Reuse without a new consequence is.

### 6. Camera movement is an attention decision

Israel–Palestine's 12–24s passage navigates across an implied collection of documents and maps, reorients them, then brings a regional map forward. Neighboring objects exist at the edges before they become subjects. This supports a larger editorial space. It does not mean every scene needs a sprawling desk or rotation.

A close crop is justified when it reveals a source detail. A pullback is justified when it introduces a relationship beyond that detail. A held frame is justified when a comparison needs reading. Slow drifting over an unchanged image does not automatically do any of these things. Recorded camera movement within sourced golf footage must not be counted as an authored animation technique.

The sampled evidence supports these positional and scale changes, but not exact easing reconstruction. A plausible original implementation would use separable source images, masks, tracked anchors, shared transforms and an explicit scene graph. That is an engineering inference, not a recovered Vox rig.

### 7. Materials establish a world; overlays select meaning

The references do not have one universal scrapbook treatment. Golf alternates documentary footage, grids, product objects and clean diagrams. Electoral uses restrained units and analytical layouts. Israel–Palestine uses a sustained archival wall. Fahrenheit combines filmed reaction, historical artifacts and simplified scales. Wildlife moves among interviews, sources, geography and a systems model.

Texture can bind a source to its material; an outline can select the relevant part; an annotation can survive a reframe by staying attached to that feature. A global dirt layer, arbitrary jitter or routine focus hunt cannot supply the missing relationship. Keep Curio's source-local paper/ink/photographic finishing and overlays. Apply focus hunts when an actual source inspection or attention transfer motivates them, then settle for reading. Protect text and the locked ending. More finishing on an unresolved composition would make the unresolved composition busier.

## Comparison of the six Curio films

Vox's landscape explainers have more horizontal space and minutes for argument; Curio's portrait shorts have central captions and much less time. The comparison therefore concerns bounded claim passages, object continuity and attention, not equal scene counts, equivalent pacing or a requirement to reproduce landscape layouts in a vertical frame. A shorter film can carry one strong relationship instead of compressing a whole Vox episode. These are analytical judgments, not viewer-comprehension test results.

These are the six latest complete exports recovered for the review, not six arbitrary examples selected for failure. A complete candidate is not an approved release. Cold Metal is a historical approved film; Sun is the current owner-adopted full-film benchmark. Mint v2, Onion v1, Phantom v1 and Black Dwarf full v2 are assessed as candidates without inventing owner approval.

### Cold Metal: 12–20s

Observed: the finger moves toward the spoon; an inset enters below, then arrows develop inside it. The following comparison page repeats spoon/hand arrangements for two materials. This is not random motion: approach and transfer have explanatory intent. The weakness is splitting the contact event from its explanation and then restarting on a fresh page.

Proposed passage: hold a clear equal-temperature baseline → show contact on the photographed spoon → attach the qualitative heat-transfer annotation to that actual contact → introduce the wood counterpart on the same comparison axis → reveal a stronger versus weaker transfer cue. Preserve what remains constant: initial conditions and direction of transfer. Do not invent measured flow rates or imply temperature is identical throughout contact. This is a proposed redesign contract, not a rendered revision of the approved film.

### Mint: 12–20s

Observed: the leaf and ice remain prominent, routes draw toward TRPM8, and the channel activates. A useful causal relationship is already present. However, attention repeatedly returns to the inputs and headline; the small receptor card must compete for reading time.

Proposed passage: establish mint as a sensory input → follow one clearly labeled input into a sufficiently large source-attached receptor inspection → hold the same receptor location while introducing cooling as the comparison input → show convergence on channel activation → separately state that this does not demonstrate an equivalent fall in tissue temperature. Preserve the same receptor and response destination. Do not suggest literal whole-leaf travel or claim an anatomical molecular animation is observed footage. The schematic remains explicitly conceptual.

### Onion: 14–22s

Observed: a reaction card yields to onion imagery; LF tokens rise; the eye enters; tokens travel toward it; the eye becomes larger. This is one of the clearest cases of an image carrying a verb. It should be developed, not dismissed.

Proposed passage: retain the cut edge while inspecting damaged cells → reveal the chemical sequence within that inspection → emerge from the same spatial anchor into the conceptual volatile route → introduce the eye along the destination direction → show the recipient consequence. Avoid resetting to the original poster just to introduce another label. Chemical steps still need accurate labeling; tokens are a schematic convention, not visible gas or a molecular scale claim.

### Phantom: 14–22s

Observed: the same pocket crop and marked area persist while headings progress from sensation to expectation to interpretation. A lower line and words name the proposed process but do little to embody it. The uncertainty labels are a strength, and the proposed explanation should not be promoted into fact for a more dramatic animation.

Proposed passage: keep one ambiguous cue visibly unchanged → distinguish the possible input from the interpretation assigned to it → present the expectation-dependent interpretation as a labeled proposed model → return to the actual phone state as the observable result. If the evidence cannot support a detailed physiological animation, use a restrained inference diagram attached to the pocket source. Keep the prevalence survey separate: a count of reports is evidence that people report the experience, not evidence establishing this mechanism. Do not fabricate recorded vibrations or experimental control data.

### Sun: 18–26s

Observed: the last-light boundary advances through the established Sun–Earth geometry; it arrives before the city darkens. That operation deserves its benchmark status. It explains delay through visible state change rather than merely adding the word 'delay.'

Proposed future adaptation: carry the source/path/destination relationship across the transition into the city consequence with fewer intervening restatements. Let the city become dominant when the question shifts from propagation to experience. Keep the diagram schematic and the hypothetical premise explicit. Do not modify the approved movie, turn Sun's exact diagonal into a channel template or discard its material, caption and audio standard.

### Black Dwarf: 30–38s

Observed: the sphere stays broadly unchanged while a coral limit line descends toward the fixed mass line in the lower figure. This is a legitimate visual explanation with a hierarchy problem. Making the star flash or spin would add spectacle without clarifying the threshold relationship.

Proposed passage: establish the remnant → inspect the support relationship attached to that remnant → allow the limit/mass comparison to occupy the dominant working area → keep the mass reference stable while the support limit falls → reconnect the crossing condition to an explicitly conditional remnant consequence. A threshold diagram is not a literal shell inside the star; use an unmistakable comparison convention. Preserve the fixed-mass correction, conditional model, special remnant subset and proton-survival caveat. Do not substitute an unsupported detailed explosion.

## Why the problem recurs despite the infrastructure

The repository already requires continuity of visual thought, image-owned operations, first/intermediate/resolved states and creative review. Another statement that 'every shot needs a purpose' will not fix noncompliance. The documents are ahead of the rendered results.

The code is not inherently incapable. For example, Black Dwarf full-v2/scenes.cjs exposes separate plate and overlay stages, reusable title/caption functions, source-local finishing and custom subject movement. It also repeatedly fixes a headline above, centered caption near y=918 and a lower explanatory area. The current implementation makes the familiar layout easy to reproduce. That is direct implementation evidence in this film, not proof that every film shares one renderer or that Canvas is the cause.

Five recurring decisions create the gap:

1. **Assets are approved as attractive isolated frames.** A later transition is expected to connect them. But connected object states may require masks, interior layers, a different camera extent and a destination that those assets cannot support.
2. **The spoken sentence becomes the unit of visual replacement.** A new title or image accompanies a line instead of an unanswered question determining how long the visual world persists.
3. **The hero is chosen by subject identity.** The star stays dominant because the story is about a star, even when a support relationship is the actual subject of the moment.
4. **The caption zone becomes a layout template.** The 42–58% rule protects reading, but it does not require an otherwise empty horizontal floor across the entire film. Compose around the actual caption bounds and platform controls; test each state. Do not move captions to the bottom to solve the art problem.
5. **Execution evidence is mistaken for creative evidence.** A manifest proving 12fps quantization, texture, overlays and focus settling cannot prove that a viewer saw why a consequence followed. Owner approval is artifact-bound, not blanket proof that future scenes work.

## Practical correction proposed for future work

These are proposed ways to enforce existing requirements. This audit does not approve a new visual system, start a proof film or silently rewrite the adopted standard.

**Begin with the hardest visual claim.** Before making a finished asset board, specify one causal sentence, what the viewer must compare, what remains fixed, and the visible change that provides the answer. Choose a passage that could otherwise collapse into a noun image and text. For Dust, that future review would need to interrogate the grain/interaction/consequence relationship, not approve a handsome grain illustration by itself. No new Dust claims or art are established here.

**Design three to five connected states in one working space.** The intermediate state must be plausible to construct and understandable. For each new element write its entry reason, exact feature anchor and exit reason. Permit an intentional cut where a shared question or matched feature bridges it; do not force every relationship into a decorative morph.

**Make asset requirements follow those states.** Inventory independent image parts, repaired hidden regions, cutaway structure, crop latitude, label anchors, source provenance and conceptual conventions. A flattened final image is inadequate when the next state needs the inside of an object. Use original assets or cleared sources; do not trace Vox compositions, reuse their screenshots or copy their brand.

**Review hierarchy at phone size before finishing.** Ask: which object receives the first look? Is that where the explanatory change occurs? Can the viewer tell what stayed constant and what changed? Is there a competing headline saying the same thing as the caption? Useful labels remain in the silent review. Not every nuanced scientific claim can be understood without language; the test checks the relationship, not compulsory wordlessness.

**Require an explicit comparison against the diagnosed failure.** A future owner-review record should say, for example, 'the support change now owns the main composition, rather than occurring in a lower card under an inert star.' A screenshot count, attractive art or an effect inventory is insufficient evidence for that statement. Only after owner art approval should the authorized moving passage test motion, sound and finishing together. Whole-film approval remains separate.

**Preserve the adopted craft after the visual argument is working.** Native 1080×1920/30fps; 12fps scene/camera; meaningful changes around 1.5–2s rather than a quota of unrelated entrances; Playfair 900; clean centered Manrope captions; source-local textures and overlays; motivated focus hunts that settle; natural Bernard; evolving original music; existing SFX first; exact London Bridge ending; immediate versioned GitHub saving. No halo behind type. Audio, facts, rights, caption timing, full-film art and export checks stay independent.

## What this study resolves, and what remains open

It establishes a source-backed diagnosis and specific repair directions for six actual exports. It shows that the missing quality is not one more overlay family or a universal collage appearance. It identifies existing successful operations worth preserving and makes the next creative review materially more specific.

It does not establish mastery of Vox, exact entrance easing, end-to-end audiovisual rhythm, current-channel coverage or audience effectiveness. Those require more direct motion/listening examination and demonstrated original work under the owner's approval gates. Kurzgesagt analysis remains separate. Dust stays paused; its existing static candidates are retained for history, not treated as the chosen visual answer. Sun remains the adopted craft benchmark while its repeated layout is open to this specific owner-requested critique.
