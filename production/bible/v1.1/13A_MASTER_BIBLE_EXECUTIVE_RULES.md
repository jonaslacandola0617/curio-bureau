# 13 — CURIO BUREAU MASTER CREATIVE, MOTION, AUDIO & PRODUCTION BIBLE
**Authority:** This is the compact, enforceable **single source of truth** for Curio Bureau after its seven-episode initial review. Detailed rationale and diagrams live in 01–12. If an older episode prompt conflicts with this Bible's October 2026 safeguards, this document takes precedence unless the user explicitly approves a revised standard.

## Section A — Fixed identity
**Channel:** Curio Bureau. **Promise:** Stories, systems, and surprises. **Mission:** Reveal the hidden decisions and physical systems behind genuine surprising events with high documentary credibility and cinematic 2D/2.5D visual explanation. Voice is curious, confident, concise, ethically restrained.

**Palette core:** navy `#0D1C2B`, ivory `#F5F0E7`, gold `#C8A26B`, coral `#D57864`. Navy/ivory form hierarchy, gold means evidence/important connection, coral means changing/danger state. Episode-subject colors allowed for world/material; do not reproduce Vox yellow-on-black branding.

**Fonts:** Playfair Display for major editorial statements and final revelation, Manrope for labels, data, captions and source info. Pin OFL font files internally, verify families, hashes and actual raster specimens, do not rely on visual resemblance or network load. Primary title 76–108 logical px, captions 42–55 logical px, support labels 18–36 logical px based on role.

**Folio:** restrained C/B mark and source/location markers, not a permanent header plus large headline plus bottom dark caption bar. **Closing:** final story geometry merges into concise Curio Bureau mark; 0.8–1.8s branded tail.

**Caption discipline:** narration phrase text centered in normalized vertical band 42–58% with dynamic in-band placement; scenes are composed AROUND its footprint. Must survive TikTok and YouTube Shorts UI masks. Actual word alignment required for FINAL; estimated captions = WIP.

## Section B — Flexible filmmaking vocabulary
**Shot families:** full-bleed physical consequence, section/exploded mechanism, object macro, human scale, photo archive, accurate map, data comparison, source-document macro, calibrated before/after, asymmetric collage, POV, chronological construction, typographic argument, environmental payoff. See 07.

**Motion choice by cause:** physical events follow physically justified timing (friction, water, turning, gravity), maps/camera use clear eased interpolation, photos use subtle cinematic refocus plus source detail, headlines track narrator emphasis. Each scene answers what changed and why. Semantic shape match preferred to generic fade, but never fake geometry just to create a morph.

**Art direction:** subject-specific environments must have recognizable detailed multi-part silhouettes: 1919 streets with period rails/carts, 1967 period cars/junctions, Chicago masonry/ratchet beams, genuine lake/mine geometry. No stick figures or undetailed block facades as major narrative objects. Premium raster illustration without matching-quality vector mechanisms is inconsistent and fails director review.

**Avoid sameness:** no >2 consecutive equivalent headline/layout/camera shots unless visibly progressive causal animation; maintain shot fingerprint ledger and test 6 consecutive compositions. Every episode needs its own unique hero physical motion and different subject-correct environment palette; do not merely recolor prior animations.

## Section C — Story architecture
1. First visible frame already contains strange action. NO black, logo, date-card pre-roll.
2. By 3s viewers understand the weird premise; by 5s one strong causal question exists.
3. One central question, 5–7 logically escalating beats: consequence → context → hidden mechanism → human/consequence → evidence → changed interpretation.
4. Research and material rights locked before full script.
5. Three materially distinct hooks with phone-mute comprehension preflight; choose by accuracy, clarity, intrigue and production feasibility.
6. No forced dramatic claims; accurate calendar/government rules, mechanics, human casualties and dates are essential.
7. Final explanation changes the meaning of first frame; integrate a short brand tag with no dead-air tail.

## Section D — Narration, sound and caption hard gates
**VOICE drives all cut points.** Record natural voice first, decode, trim genuine boundaries and lock one WAV as timing authority. Normal transition 0.12–0.55s; unintended >0.85s is editorial FAIL; >1.20s between voice passages is absolute release FAIL. Measure within clips, not only between clips. Never fill 8–14s fixed visual windows with 3–5s clips. No eSpeak, forced unnatural tempo, mispronounced names or missing final word. Final voice tail ≤1.8s normally.

**SOUND:** separate VOICE/MUSIC/AMBIENCE/TRANSITIONS/DETAIL/IMPACTS buses, original/cleared real instrumentation with perceptible melodic structure and physically synchronized SFX, critically listen on phone and headphones. Target -14 to -12 LUFS integrated and ≤-1 dBTP true peak, with 48k stereo. LUFS is necessary technical data, not creative approval. Maintain asset licenses.

**CAPTIONS:** Manrope, 1–2 lines central band, actual final voice word-aligned and checked with platform masks on final MP4 and WebM. Never leave a missing `WordBoundary` unreported or call syllable-estimated timing final.

## Section E — Image and archival integrity
Only cite historical images actually cleared for the intended use. Never generate fake archival photographs or newspapers with invented words. Author original simulations as reconstruction (and label when ambiguity possible). Maps use credible physical locations; numeric claim uses source and context. Graphs/sections cannot invent exact stress, flow or scale values.

## Section F — Mandatory two-format native 4K delivery
Primary authoritative video: **2160×3840 30fps H.264 MP4** encoded with high-quality native vector/PDF/SVG/Canvas frames, AAC ≥~320kbps stereo 48k. Second authoritative file: **2160×3840 30fps VP9 WebM**, Opus 256–320kbps stereo 48k. Burn captions into BOTH; also supply final SRT, original interactive HTML studio and research/QA/stems as project files.

Native-4K PNG sequence is the pipeline gold standard: each 2160×3840 frame rendered individually from high-resolution originals, **not an upscaled 1080p movie**. High-quality JPEG transfer and pixel-upscaled art should be recorded as deviations and cannot pass strict gold-standard rendering QA. Verify actual pixel quality at 100% in both encodes, not only ffprobe width.

## Section G — Twelve gate production process
(1) source inventory → (2) fact lock → (3) 3 hooks/5s animatics → (4) final natural voice and word timings → (5) storyboard+shot fingerprints → (6) 5 full-quality style frames → (7) complete lower-resolution timed animatic → (8) subject-specific assets/license check → (9) full motion + stems/mix → (10) 1080p creative PRE-4K review → (11) lossless native 4K MP4/WebM → (12) full encoded/pixel/audio/phone UI QA.

**P0 release fail:** unsupported fact, unlicensed crucial archive, truncated media, wrong 4K resolution, missing 48k stereo, broken captions, app UI occlusion, >1.2s voice gap, absent music/voice/effects, illegible type, final-font fallback. **P1 creative fail:** generic placeholder graphics, repeated empty cards, bland static first frame, huge illustration-quality discontinuity, no meaningful animated mechanism, repetitive shot compositions, score feels like inaudible tone, uninspiring payoff. **P2 polish:** fine-grain consistency, kerning, historical footnotes and minor texture distinctions.

Final status can ONLY be 'FINAL MASTER' after creator reviews **entire actual final MP4 and WebM at normal speed**, checks actual voice stem, knows rights and verifies technical + creative gates. Source code running or FFmpeg success by itself is insufficient.
