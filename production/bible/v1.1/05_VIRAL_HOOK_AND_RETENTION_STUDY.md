## Three hook alternatives for each locally evidenced episode

### Samoa
- A — Impact: Dec 29 calendar rips into Dec 31 in 0.0–0.6s. Spoken: 'This country deleted a Friday.' 1–3s location boundary; 3–5s 'How can a date disappear?'.
- B — Human: worker flips a wall calendar and the next date is missing; phone safe, clear. 1–3s reveal it's nationwide, not a broken calendar. The opening is an original reconstruction.
- C — Paradox: same instant displayed as Thursday on one side, Saturday on the other; crossing date-line reveals governance explanation.

### Lake Peigneur
- A — Spectacle: recognizable boat slides toward intense central sink; white foam and shore already moving; 1–3s reveal industrial drilling and hidden mine.
- B — Mystery: water drains under cutaway lake bed; the visual itself reveals underground emptiness but not source.
- C — Human/scale: overhead barge crew reaction as shoreline collapses (anonymous stylized reconstruction); verify no fictional witnesses presented as record.

### Boston
- A — Contradiction: street already carrying viscous dark flood; word 'MOLASSES' enters after viewer thinks water.
- B — Cause: larger-than-human riveted seam tearing into industrial wave; angle expands to street.
- C — Consequence: trolley/elevated steel bends under moving dark wave, then source tank and 2.3M measurement.

### Sweden

## Test protocol (actual evidence rather than virality claims)
1. Produce 3 executable five-second animatics **per new episode** in identical 1080×1920 phone format. Keep historical accuracy equivalent.
2. Score comprehension at 0.2, 1.0 and 3.0s in mute viewing; ask blinded reviewers 'what physically happened?' and 'what question do you want answered?'.
3. Score: visual clarity (30%), surprising contradiction (25%), unanswered question (20%), emotional or human stakes (15%), confidence in accuracy (10%). A preferred creator aesthetic does not automatically win.
4. Once live, use platform-native retention metrics. Record first 3-second hold, average view duration, completion rate, first drop-off time, loop/replay ratio, traffic source and cohort, with comparable upload window. **Do not substitute public views for retention.**
5. Prefer clean controlled tests across multiple uploads where platform supports it; avoid concluding causality from one viral hit.

## Strategy
One premise + one genuinely novel visual + one unresolved causal question. **Never use a blank channel-title intro.** Open with consequence before chronology, but reveal material context early enough to be intelligible and truthful. Avoid adopting Zack D Films' 3D/spectacle style; transfer the short story mechanics into original Vox-like 2D/2.5D visual journalism.
