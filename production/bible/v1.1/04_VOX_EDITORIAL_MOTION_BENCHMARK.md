## Producer-validated workflow principles
- [Vox Atlas producer Sam Ellis interview, Storybench](https://www.storybench.org/vox-atlas-producer-sam-ellis-on-his-map-animations/) describes animated maps integrated with sourced photo/video and data, and the journalistic idea of using geography to answer what words leave unclear. This is stronger evidence of production method than generic 'Vox style' tutorials.
- [Vox video team production overview](https://glasp.co/youtube/DiheSWIxydk) explicitly identifies producer, researchers, editors and animation specialists; our process should have *distinct sign-off gates* even when one person performs several roles.
- [Johnny Harris on maps](https://aescripts.com/learn/post/how-johnny-harris-makes-maps) describes developing map assets and GEOlayers/After Effects workflow over years, including consistent source layers plus camera control.
- [How Vox-Style Edits Are Built](https://earnedits.com/how-vox-style-edits-are-built/) is an independent study, **not an official Vox production manual**. Use its highlighter/maps/kinetic-type observations cautiously.
- [TypeFlow technique overview](https://www.typeflow.studio/blog/vox-style-explainer-videos), 2026, likewise an independent analysis. Its narrative-driven highlighter premise is transferable, but not a license to reuse Vox graphic designs.

## Technique transfer matrix — source method → animation primitive → quality test
| Reference method | Curio primitive | Time/interaction concept | Asset prerequisite | Do not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Map camera | Geographic hierarchy with data-driven boundaries | 0.35–0.8s reframe, then meaningful new overlay | credible coastline/street files | arbitrary island blob |
| Archival macro | Full-bleed sourced scan, citation strip, yellow/gold annotation | 0.2–0.5s exact phrase underline when VO reaches it | rights-checked 2K+ image | screenshot as decorative postage stamp |
| Object-to-map morph | Convert road marking/river/screw thread contour to line on map | shape-match ±8px at transition seam | vector physical silhouette + map polyline | repeated generic dissolve |
| Mechanism explode | Separate 3–5 engineered parts one at a time | each beat 0.35–1.2s, pauses for comprehension | annotated multi-part asset, real dimensions | rectangle stands in for industrial object |
| Detail-to-scale reveal | Macro cause, whip/cut to consequential environment | 0.4s–1.1s, anchor object persists | two designed angles in matching coordinates | show giant empty navy field |
| Document-to-argument | highlight source quote then visualize its physical consequence | time to audio word onset | verifiable original quote and annotated source | invented text / fake archival page |
| Source ambiguity | measured double annotation: '1919 account' vs 'modern reconstruction' | distinct cards, precise year labels | claim-level evidence log | show hypothetical crack path as a real photo |
| Before/after | locked camera with moving difference mask | change causes visible geometric deviation | same calibrated physical map at both states | two unrelated flat rectangles |
| Visual metaphor | date square vanishes / road arrow changes side | instantaneous first-frame physical surprise | truthful metaphor supported by data | fake historical spectacle |
| Movement across depth | 2.5D layered street / cutaway | parallax factor by depth plane, no false physics | at least 4 designed depth layers | one large image zoomed repeatedly |

## Applying Vox principles without cloning Vox
Use the higher-order system: **one researched question; information appears as evidence; frame layout is chosen to show that evidence; camera movement reveals a cause; graphic components are original to Curio Bureau**. Keep Playfair and Manrope rather than switching to Vox's typefaces. Avoid signature imitation, yellow branding, copied title cards, rights-unclear media and misleading reconstruction.

**Formal gap:** A claimed 10-video *shot-by-shot* Vox study is NOT complete until these real videos are legally viewed/decoded and their precise 0–5s/transition frames logged. This document is a curated and evidence-graded benchmark pack, not an invented observational transcript.
