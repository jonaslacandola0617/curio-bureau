# 09 — STORYTELLING & HOOK BIBLE
## Editorial signature: consequence → hidden system → human stakes → clear, earned payoff
Curio Bureau follows a mystery without abandoning evidence: show *the impossible-looking consequence*, then explain the structural or geographical system that made it possible. Each short needs **one central question** and an answer that changes how the viewer understands the first frame. Not every opening must involve danger; the surprise may be a missing date, an unbuilt foundation, a changed social rule or an astonishing scale.

## 1. Four proof-bearing story spines
- **Physical reversal:** expected flow of matter or objects contradicts intuition; explain underlying system. Lake Peigneur, Boston.
- **Rule reversal:** a policy or time convention visibly changes everyday life. Samoa, Dagen H.
- **Hidden infrastructure:** surprising physical feat is necessary because the original system has a flaw. Chicago.
- **Evidence puzzle:** an archival image/document contradicts a common belief; transform evidence into diagram rather than unverified reenactment. Suitable future episodes.

A new film selects one dominant spine; secondary spines are allowed if not muddying the question.

## 2. First five seconds as a working script
| Time | Story job | Image job | Voice job | Audio job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.7s | Surprise is already underway | recognizable action, single subject, high clarity | first phrase begins quickly | distinctive physical sound, not stock whoosh |
| 0.7–1.8s | Plain-language contrast | consequence becomes legible | short subject+verb, not preamble | support, clear diction |
| 1.8–3.0s | Provide minimum context | reveal unique object, location or scale | clarify impossible-looking claim | restrained accent |
| 3.0–5.0s | Create causal question | reveal hint of mechanism, withhold resolution | explicit/implicit 'How could that happen?' | introduce musical question motif |

**Disqualifiers:** intro logo, 'In the year...' prologue, blank frame, multiple simultaneous mysteries, precise claim unsupported by research, false reenactment, montage with no one readable event.

## 3. Six viewer-carrying turns for a ~75s film
- 0–5: why watch, physically.
- 5–14: where/when and what is unexpectedly at stake; maps or reality.
- 14–28: hidden system / contradiction.
- 28–45: key mechanism and turning point; show genuinely animated complex structure, not held slide.
- 45–60: humans, investigation, or second-order effect; bring documentary evidence into foreground.
- 60–75: earned reversal/outcome + brief final mark; often return to first visual element from a newly informed viewpoint.
These are **story beat estimates**, not an excuse to pad narration. The film must FOLLOW the real voice track; scenes may shorten/extend when there is enough new content.

## 4. Retention micro-promises
At each act transition write two columns: `What the viewer currently thinks` → `What the next visual changes`. If the new scene does not change knowledge, remove or combine it. Avoid continuous question-slinging; the audience needs partial answers and escalating stakes. A reveal is not merely a label flying in.

## 5. Episode-specific examples / improvements
- **001:** cold open with 30th of month disappearing; promise 'can a country skip a calendar date?' After explanation of International Date Line, show human scheduling consequence; no claim time itself vanished.
- **004:** shoreline collapse then mine; don't falsely portray full documented footage. At 35s human evacuation shows real consequence. 52 miners all escaped is accurate as historical source; never infer identities.
- **005:** brown wave hits street before identifying molasses; the hidden answer is weak riveted infrastructure and ignored reports, not 'syrup is funny'. 21 deaths demand sober transition.
- **006:** driver/road switch at 04:50 under traffic restriction; provide advisory 1955 vote, 1963 law, 05:00 rule, 06:00 staged traffic without implying all drivers crossed at once.
- **007:** actual screw handle moves, whole ornate storefront rises slightly; why? sewer and higher street grade; no entire city rose simultaneously. Compare 1860 Lake Street and 1861 Tremont House separately.

## 6. Hook workshop template (three variants before production)
For each of A consequence / B mechanism / C human-or-data contradiction:
`FACT-LOCK / FRAME0 OBJECT / CAMERA POSITION / VISIBLE MOTION <0.5s / FIRST 13 WORDS / 2.5s CONTEXT / 5s OPEN QUESTION / SOUND ACCENT / COPYRIGHT STATUS / MUTE COMPREHENSION / FAILURE RISK`.
Three alternative hooks should look materially different—not three headlines over the same fixed frame. Choose after 3–5 independent phone-size mute reviewers can identify the physical action.

## 7. What not to infer from social-video public counts
High views can reflect creator reach, topic, packaging, paid distribution or algorithm conditions. Views are **not** measured retention. Private YouTube/TikTok analytics are required to answer which exact 1–5s frames make viewers stay; use hypotheses, A/B-ish tests and coded post-mortems until those metrics become accessible.

## 8. Outro
No generic 'subscribe' before factual resolution. Last spoken clause should answer the first question while revealing a larger system. Integrate 'Curio Bureau' for a short 0.8–1.8s branded tail on a still-moving appropriate environment; make rewatch loop ethical, not confusing. Sound must conclude just after the final word, never 4–5s later.
