**Current authority: v1.1, adopted 11 October 2026.**

## Hard rules and inheritance
- Identity: Curio Bureau, **Stories, systems, and surprises.** Playfair Display for major editorial expression; Manrope for captions/utility. Core palette navy **#0D1C2B**, ivory **#F5F0E7**, gold **#C8A26B**, coral **#D57864**; subject-specific accents allowed.
- Center captions in the platform-safe vertical **42–58%** band where composition permits, design around them, verify phone-size TikTok and YouTube Shorts UI masks, and precisely align to recorded words.
- Voice-first pacing: normal transitions ~**0.12–0.55s**; unplanned >**0.85s** requires editorial correction; >**1.20s** between passages fails release. Inspect isolated stem **inside** clips as well as between clips; do not hide pauses with music.
- Natural voice and actual audible structured music, authentic/source-tracked Foley/SFX, separate buses, critical listening; no robotic fallback, inaudible score, or placeholder-effects-as-final.
- Both delivery **H.264/AAC MP4 and VP9/Opus WebM must be true native 2160×3840 at 30fps**, not upscaled or mislabeled. Validate actual codecs, frames, sound, captions, first/last frames, fonts, and sharpness.
- Creative consistency in craft, evidence, and type **but not duplicated layouts**: unique subject-specific hero mechanism; multi-part recognizable drawings; no repetitive center cards/undetailed objects; inspect six-shot visual fingerprints and quality continuity.
- Use true rights-cleared archives, real locations/maps and sourced claims; never pass reconstructed images off as historical photographs or actual footage.
- **Do not generate a video unless the user specifically requests video production.** A read/ingest/analyze request triggers documents-only study, not scripts/visuals/rendering.

## Evidence constraints
The initial audit is **evidence-qualified**, not a claim to have watched every reference in its entirety: the exact Episodes 001–003 masters were unavailable; an alternate Samoa pilot/rebuild was sampled. External Vox/viral comparisons drew on public metadata and production discussions, not full external frame decoding. Real audience-retention data was unavailable. Future analyses should update these limitations when verified sources become available; no invented timings, audience metrics, or font-forensic conclusions.
