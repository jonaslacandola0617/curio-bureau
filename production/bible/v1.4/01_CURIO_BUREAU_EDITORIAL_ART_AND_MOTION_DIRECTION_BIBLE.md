## 0. Creative premise and priority
**Curio Bureau — “Stories, systems, and surprises.”** Original premium short factual documentaries, using beautiful and specific documentary art, imagery, object inspection, geography, diagrams, comparison and intentional motion. The channel's identity is *not* Vox's logo, stock transitions or a paper-texture preset. The objective is the high-quality editorial reasoning embodied in the user's supplied five Vox films, with original Curio subjects and design.

A moving scene must represent a developing **visual argument**. The viewer should understand why the next shot follows, why an object or label changes, and what new information the movement provides.

**Governing maxim: continuity of visual thought.** One shot may inherit an object, location, category, shape, measurement, evidence, visual question or position from another. That inheritance can be communicated through a clean editorial cut, spatial navigation, a physical reveal, a diagram change or a matched move. It does **not** require an uninterrupted camera move or perpetual morphing.

**Governing motion contract:** **KNOWN STATE → MEANINGFUL OPERATION → NEWLY READABLE STATE → HOLD / NEXT QUESTION.** An animation without a concrete answer to “What does the viewer understand now?” is not editorially justified. The resolved state and readable hold are as important as the entrance.

## PART II — ART DIRECTION AND REPRESENTATION

### 4. Assign each piece of artwork a narrative role
Different visual elements do different jobs:
- **Recognizable object/photograph/illustration:** establish the subject's identity, era, material, scale and specific distinguishing characteristics.
- **Document/source:** provide evidence, authentic wording or a valid historical trace, with visible attribution and proper rights.
- **Geographic map:** establish location, distance, historical positioning, change across eras, or a real spatial relationship.
- **Chart/comparison/diagram:** expose quantitative difference, organization, input/output, process or internal mechanism.
- **Annotation:** temporarily select what viewers should inspect; track the object/source precisely.
- **Typography:** identify proposition, unit, date, category or concluding insight with deliberate hierarchy.
- **Paper/film/material treatment:** establish a cohesive physical or archival environment where that material is genuinely relevant, not an all-purpose filter.

A dense collage remains clear if the current reading task and relative hierarchy are controlled. An empty minimalist composition can still be a poor explanatory design. Minimalism refers to **the number of simultaneous interpretive tasks**, not the number of vertices in a historical object.

### 5. Mixed-media collage must be composed, not scattered
Design **recognizable hero silhouette**, deliberate unequal scale, intentional overlap and contact/shadow/lighting coherence. A tank cutout with material folds, a cropped annotated source, and a map can work together only when their relationships earn the space they occupy. Every layer should contribute relevant evidence, relationship, scale, atmosphere or chronology.

Photo borders and paper edges should have material logic. A label on a physical print follows the print if it moves. A sourced clipping stays attributable. A torn-paper edge is not a license to float each source in an unrelated rectangle. A book page has its own typography. A technical map does not need the shadow and torn border of a photograph.

### 6. Art quality: source specificity over automatic complexity
**Owner correction #2 — mandatory.** No method is banned purely because it is procedural. Canvas, SVG, HTML, JavaScript, shaders, compositing, raster illustration, actual licensed archival imagery and image generation can all produce excellent work. Accept or reject **specificity, construction, intent, legibility, craftsmanship and truthful explanatory purpose**—not “was it coded?”

Deliberate simplification is often GOOD. Introduce a detailed photo or mechanically specific recreation, then transform it to a clean schematic that isolates the one part required to understand the mechanism. The schematic may use fewer shapes than the photograph but remain a better representation for that task. The failure is an **inaccurate generic placeholder** (blocks claiming to depict a period-specific engine, tank or radio) that no longer represents the features needed for recognition.

Detailed imagery that serves as historical/physical evidence must not be downgraded simply because easier shapes animate more conveniently. A visually refined chart may properly be crisp and spare; a historical cutout should maintain credible period/material characteristics. **Craft and explanatory function**, not pixel detail everywhere, establish consistency.

### 7. Distinct art and explanatory modes (switch as reasoning demands)
| Mode | Primary job | Material expectations | Typical movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documentary/evidence | Recognition and truthful source context | Licensed photography, actual readable document, source-specific recreation | Framing, selection, highlight, direct cut |
| Physical object | Exterior/parts/material/mechanism | Specific silhouette, parts, realistic contact, optional 3D or sectional model | Rotate, open, isolate, explode, cross-section |
| Analytical field | Process, communication, input/rule/output, organizations | Restrained crisp forms, grounded anchors, accurate lines/labels | Build relationship in a justified order |
| Geographic | Place, era, distribution, path, position | Historic-accurate sourced map or marked schematic | Map focus, reveal real overlay, scale/era change |
| Quantitative | Counts, shared scale, ratio, distribution | Equal units or honest axes, labels/legend/denominator | Stage data, reclassify symbols, **hold** |
| Spatial archive | Explore adjacent photographs, source plates, artifacts and evidence | Real independent foreground/mid/background masks and repaired hidden imagery | Short selective camera navigation and occlusion |

The same episode may use several; do not force all into identical backgrounds or fixed palettes. Brand cohesion comes from type, semantic color, quality, contrast, pacing and intellectual continuity.

### 8. Type, color and attention
**Identity:** Playfair Display editorial titles and Manrope functional type, maps, values and centered caption phrases, with verified actual font loading. Authentic source typography remains source-native.

**Color:** Curio's navy \`#0D1C2B\`, ivory \`#F5F0E7\`, gold \`#C8A26B\`, coral \`#D57864\` are core semantic roles. Historical materials may have independent authentic accents. Category colors persist; attention colors identify the active item briefly. A “false/implied” group must be distinguishable from “actual” by labeling and fill/outline as well as hue. Highlights should usually appear *after* the source or context is established, and stop when their job ends.

Avoid huge poetic headings over every image, repeated "Fieldnotes 01–04" furniture, universal typewriter animation, per-character text movement everywhere or grain/fringing that weakens phone-size labels. Preserve center-safe Manrope captions normally within vertical 42–58% with the established tasteful rounded translucent navy/gold-edged treatment. Source/hero shots must be composed *around* caption safe area.

## PART III — STORY FIRST: CORRECTED MANDATORY PRODUCTION ORDER

### 9. Owner correction #1: editorial sequence BEFORE finished artwork
The earlier proposed ordering—polished stills, *then* detailed scene-direction contract—was wrong. It leads to pretty independent posters whose assets cannot physically or editorially support the planned transformation. This correction is now binding.

**Production order for all new complex motion sequences:**

**STEP 1 — Story and factual evidence.** One actual viewer question; one-sentence thesis; sourced beat list; evidence, hypothesis and reconstruction statuses; expected initial model and desired final understanding. Name what the film is changing in the audience's beliefs.

**STEP 2 — Editorial shot sequence / relationship map BEFORE finished artwork.** Develop more than one visual concept for hard facts. Decide why source image, object inspection, map, chart, archival workspace or direct footage serves each idea. Plan adjacent transitions: what physical or conceptual anchor will carry across? Where does one visual *form* give way to another? What would an intentional clean cut say? Reject superficial spectacle at the storyboard stage.

**STEP 3 — Motion/reading choreography and representative states.** For each shot define first, intermediate and resolved compositions and reading holds; identify initial object position, invariant, primary eye path, supporting elements, source/annotation anchors, screen-centered subtitles, momentum, camera path and how the exit is motivated. Plan a narrative beat *sequence*, not a set of independent poster prompts. Record the actual reason for every transition.

**STEP 4 — Purpose-built asset construction contract.** THEN specify the individual artwork to create/obtain. Explicitly define layers needed for camera occlusion, foreground pass, photo-to-diagram transformation, physical reveal, annotated source and matched cuts. Specify actual resolution at maximum zoom, separately animated parts, rights/citations and background pixels that will become visible during movement.

**STEP 5 — Produce, assess and refine premium source artwork.** Commission/illustrate/license the selected objects, documents, maps and design components *for those planned shots and transitions*. Review their artistic quality, source specificity, usefulness to the argument and actual transformability. Reject a beautifully flattened still that cannot be used for the agreed choreography, just as reject a mechanically convenient but ugly image.

**STEP 6 — Rig and animate.** Separate real material planes where appropriate, reconstruct hidden regions, establish source-local anchors, masks, occlusion and camera. Create analytical vector operations in source coordinates, screen-space typography, staged data changes and truthful map overlays.

**STEP 7 — Review the MOVING ART.** Screen actual independently rendered first/intermediate/resolved frames, transitions at full speed and phone size, under/above captions. Does visual craft remain as strong as needed at each *representation*? Does motion communicate the idea? Is any shot merely a presentation slide? Reject internally; fix the concept, asset or composition before adding finishing effects.

**STEP 8 — Narration/audio + finishing.** Time event points to real audio, not fixed 3-second slide slots. Compose emotional voice, music, ambience and purposeful synchronized physical cues. Use grain/jitter/flicker/edge boil/chromatic separation sparingly after artwork is strong.

**STEP 9 — Export & independent release QA.** H.264/AAC MP4 and VP9/Opus WebM at true native detail and governing 4K/30fps rate for real final films, actual encoded narration/music/SFX, word-verified captions. Human creative review is a separate pass criterion.

The original 5 styleframe standard still applies: it now comes **after** editorial/shot design and includes meaningful intermediate states, not only final static pretty shots.

### 10. Required scene-direction contract
Before final art for each key beat record:
1. Narrative proposition + viewer question.
2. Source/evidence status and trust/rights.
3. Chosen representation, plus why stronger than rejected alternatives.
4. Initial composition and primary first eye target.
5. Invariant: object, geography, baseline, date, unit, category or question that must persist.
6. Three to six staged information states: **what enters, changes, remains, clears**.
7. Camera vs object vs graphic vs screen overlay movement, timing, direction, momentum and support action.
8. Readable resolved frame with explicit **recognition/inference/reading hold**.
9. Exact concept/narration relationship (word timing verified later from recorded audio).

12. Factual, map, material, caption and artistic failure checks.
If the art is hard to draw but narratively right, invest in appropriate artwork rather than substituting an unrelated easy procedural shape.

## PART IV — DEPTH, CAMERA, AND COMPOSITING

### 11. Three spatial-depth systems
**A. Flat analytical depth** — stable chart and label coordinate system where design hierarchy/contrast/order make quantities legible.

**B. Dimensional-object depth** — physically credible shape, material and multi-part construction where a measured rotation, section or zoom reveals an actual object relationship.

**C. Navigable archival depth** — distinct documents, maps, photographs and artifacts coherently sharing a 2.5D world with overlapped planes and repaired occluded areas.

These are different editorial requirements. Never apply the same camera movement or compositing rig to all three by default.

### 12. Selective parallax and source-space choreography
T09 shows foreground, middle, background preparation, Clone Stamp/hidden-pixel repair, actual depth layers and camera movement. Authentic parallax occurs because independently positioned planes respond differently to the virtual camera, not because one flattened JPG globally scales.

Use it for discovering a material object behind another, navigating a justified shared source environment, entering a historical street/collage or moving from exterior to meaningful detail. **Do not** pan continuously just so every shot moves; don't separate a flat chart into arbitrary z-planes. A little stationary time is often required for evidence reading.

The user explicitly proposed **objects passing close by or past the camera to motivate a transition**. This can be excellent when the subject is already part of the environment, occlusion has material coherence and the next representation fits. It is an *option*, not a prescribed template.

**Source-local geometry:** \`screen = viewport * camera * parent/source-object * local-point\`. Hold object labels, circles, map/source highlights to the exact intended points. Screen-space central captions remain in a separate overlay system. Verify real occluder sorting and no duplicated/collapsing planes or exposed holes. If parallax is not warranted, a restrained 2D transform or direct cut is preferable.

### 13. Representational transformations and meaningful cuts
Choose the technique based on the explanatory relation:
- **Detailed image → clean schematic** when identity/orientation must survive but detail would obscure the mechanism.
- **Exterior → section/interior** for physical parts/causal explanation.
- **Data symbol → reclassified symbol** when category or measure changes; preserve fixed positions/baselines.
- **Photo/source selection → redrawn map** when historical geography needs independent analytical labeling.
- **Source/destination graph** when input, pathway and outcome are the claim; animate relationship only after both are established.
- **Matched position/speed cut (T06)** when two different images share real directional motion and the visual handoff helps explain the next concept. Cut near the **actual velocity peak** if pursuing that effect, not assumed at the timeline midpoint.
- **Foreground-material occlusion** when a photographed/illustrated existing object can pass lens plausibly and reveal the next state.
- **Hard cut** when switching time, evidence type, setting or thought clearly; never force an unsupported causal morph.

**Do not make false causal proof:** a radio cable becoming a Rhine river might be clever graphics but may suggest historical linkage that has not been established. Direct cuts can be cleaner and more truthful. “Seamlessness” describes logic first, optical transition second.

## PART V — EDITORIAL ATTENTION AND ANIMATION PERSONALITY

### 14. Reasoning operations and holds
The primary moving element must have one job: establish, select, reveal, compare, combine, accumulate, shift context, explain cause, show output or revise the prior model. Supporting elements have lower temporal and color priority.

Plan recognition time (identify image), inference time (understand relationship) and reading time (absorb exact labels), **not only duration of the action**. If the comparison is informative, let it hold. An animated event may be a half-second reveal followed by several seconds of apparent stillness. Backgrounds rarely need autonomous drifting, constant pulsing or ornamental loops.

### 15. Choose different motion personalities as the story demands
- **Investigative / exploratory:** camera traverses a meaningful shared archival environment, selects a document, stops to read.
- **Analytical:** stable baselines, truthful numerical endpoints, accurate category/denominator switch.
- **Procedural/systemic:** reveal entities, then their links/constraints, then the combined mechanism.
- **Material/mechanical:** source-specific parts, hinges, contacts, rotation, valves, openings, cross-sections.
- **Comparative/geographic:** preserve frame of reference and one chosen selection, then show changed evidence on the same map.
- **Editorial/urgent:** quickly frame recognition, restrained deliberate move and settle, then cut to a result.

The five films do not establish universal spring constants, easing curves or pixel movement rates. Variation arises from the narrative cognitive task, not random adjacent preset changes.

### 16. Technical tutorials: T01–T10 as tools, not creative instructions
- **T01:** modest film/exposure variation, jitter and grain where a source material calls for an optical finish.
- **T02:** hand-drawn line/contour construction, Trim Paths and fine/broad Roughen Edges separation; edge boil locally attached to its source, never gratuitously across the whole frame.
- **T03:** choose and synchronize believable material Foley/transition sound, not a whoosh on everything; soundtrack referenced by tutorial is not fully independently auditioned.
- **T04:** one gradient may feed coordinated color/darkening and blur on selected geography; useful when selection is the point. If it sacrifices geographic comprehension, omit.
- **T05:** editor, designer and storyteller coordinate picture/source/meaning before cosmetic details.
- **T06:** source/target objects share a movement and cut at registered actual peak speed; not mandatory.
- **T07:** source/article presentation and highlight evidence with readable provenance; background textures do not become false proof.
- **T08:** restrained selective text/character animation (not universal typewriter).
- **T09:** real layered photo separation and background repair for parallax.
- **T10:** asymmetric arrival/settling, subtle grain/chromatic treatment and optional local 12-fps posterization. This does NOT change final 30-fps release cadence.

All After Effects / Photoshop implementation details must be adapted and independently visually tested if implemented in browser HTML/Canvas/SVG/JS. Exact Vox proprietary project rigs and soundtrack settings have NOT been recovered.

### 17. Sound as scene meaning
Voice emotional delivery should change with curiosity, danger, explanation, relief and revelation rather than static speed/pitch. Music must actually be audible, evolve tension and release, and fit source genre/period; not merely a sinusoid under the entire clip. Tactile sound and radio/mechanical noise should follow real actions and remain quiet during heavy reading; one meaningful impact is stronger than impacts on every label.

No soundtrack cues may be falsely attributed to Vox originals without a real independent listening analysis. Existing Curio SFX libraries remain governed by rights/cue sheets. Normal narration gaps, caption boundaries, true peak/loudness and original full-mix QA remain unchanged.

## PART VI — SOURCE HONESTY, MAPS AND QUANTITIES

### 18. Documentary source and reconstructive art ethics
A genuine archival image must carry valid rights/credit and correct event/era. A generated period-looking photograph is an **illustrative reconstruction**, never a hidden authentic source. Source passages remain readable and in context; selecting one line cannot remove important qualifications. Cite historical dates, quotations, scale and number claims with verified research.

### 19. Maps as independent evidence
Use valid sources for geographic positions, period names, era boundaries, route, political category and time. Distinguish actual controlled areas, proposal, claim, distribution, combat position and fictional schematic. Do not pretend artistic red route or generic Rhine curve represents a specific 1945 deployment. In a historic map-to-modern overlay keep stable orientation and labels; identify meaningful legend changes before area fill shifts. Source-attached marks remain grounded during camera travel.

### 20. Data/comparison integrity
Use common denominators, aligned baselines, consistent unit sizes and honest quantitative encodings. A chart's bar position may change because data changes—not because a camera zoom made it appear to grow. A tally consisting of 11 filled and 300 outline equal-size 100-person squares is a valid ORIGINAL way to illustrate a rounded 1,100 vs 30,000 claim **if historical context, approximation and distinct “actual vs simulated” categories are identified**; it is not a prescribed Vox chart and not a mandate to build every story as square arrays. A visually attractive infographic that fails to explain the requested relation still fails.

## PART VIII — ARTISTIC, TECHNICAL AND GOVERNANCE ACCEPTANCE

### 24. Hard artistic preflight BEFORE expensive production
Require independent scrutiny:
1. **Story:** The audience's mental model changes. Scene doesn't merely contain an object named in narration.
2. **Specific visual world:** Source images, drawn forms, equipment and materials are high-craft for their roles. No generic period machinery, plastic-looking stickmen or toy city placeholders.
3. **Sequence planned before art:** The layer plan supports intended transitions, masks, revealing intermediary states and camera paths.
4. **Actual moving result:** first, intermediate, last, transition and reading-hold frames all intentional and compelling at 9:16 phone size.
5. **True continuity:** the edit passes meaning along an invariant or makes a deliberate break in context; not one page in/page out and not constant parallax.
6. **Reading/accuracy:** source remains anchored; fake archive information excluded; quant/map endpoints correct and understandable.
7. **Audio:** narrational performance and cueing add storytelling rather than compensate for empty visuals.
8. **Truthful status:** coded/animated/rendered/technically verified does not mean artistic approval.

### 25. Standard deliverables on authorized production
- Narrative/claims/evidence ledger and story concept.
- Editorial beatboard / alternatives / shot-to-shot visual-thought connections.
- Purpose-built art specification with semantic masks/components, licensing and accurate source text.
