## 1. FIXED BRAND SYSTEM AND UNBREAKABLE RELEASE CONTRACT

**Identity:** CURIO BUREAU / *Stories, systems, and surprises.*

**Semantic core colors:** Midnight navy `#0D1C2B`, ivory `#F5F0E7`, muted gold `#C8A26B`, restrained coral `#D57864`. Historical subject accents may add materials/environment palettes; never flatten all environments into one four-color illustration style. Keep enough channel signature in the typography, typography motion, annotation and evidence treatment.

**Fonts:** Actual, verified **Playfair Display** for large editorial headings, significant dates, conceptual statements and insight; **Manrope** for captions, numeric/data text, maps, source and diagram labels. Do not claim fonts loaded because CSS specifies them. Await actual font loading, check effective face/metrics and inspect export pixels. Never distribute font binaries to users.

**Aspect and master:** 9:16, **2160×3840 native** at **30fps** for BOTH separate downloadable masters. MP4 = H.264/yuv420p with high-quality native-rendered picture, 48kHz AAC stereo (normally ~320 kb/s). WebM = VP9 / 48kHz Opus stereo. The primary MP4 must NOT be 1080p while WebM is 4K. Upscaled 1080p artwork is not native-detail 4K just because container pixels read 2160×3840. Keep sufficiently resolved bitmaps and vector text at maximum camera magnification. Lossless full-resolution source frames are the default authoritative pipeline; JPEG intermediary frames are not a substitute for a lossless PNG frame master unless distinctly approved and disclosed.

**Captions:** Burn Manrope short, appropriately sized phrase captions into both exports, centered horizontally and normally around **vertical 42–58%** of frame, respecting TikTok and YouTube Shorts UI masks. This is a COMPOSITION rule: construct the story around the reserved band so text does not obscure hands, faces, vital graphs or source evidence. Caption timings must come from verified actual recorded spoken word/phrase boundaries. Estimated captions = WIP, never FINAL. Export `.srt` too. Audit all caption configurations in actual encoded phone-scaled frames with UI mock overlays; avoid platform obstructions, including button stacks.

**Voice:** Human-sounding, emotionally shaped documentary delivery; verified names and pronunciations. Determine all scene and transition times from **the actual recorded speech**, not predetermined 8/12/14s visual slots. Normal inter-phrase gaps **0.12–0.55s**; unplanned >**0.85s** fails editorial QA; >**1.20s** between passages fails RELEASE even if a score masks the silence. Check waveform silence within recording clips, not only segment boundaries, and critically audition the complete encoded audio. Do not solve by accelerating speech or stretching it to fill scenes. Last spoken word should flow into a brief ~0.8–1.8s brand tail, not an accidental 4–5s void.

**Music and effects:** Audible, original or cleared melodically structured score (not a barely audible sine pad) with scene-specific harmonic development. Realistic, appropriately scaled and synchronized SFX/ambience for on-screen action, provenance and license recorded, independent VOICE/MUSIC/AMBIENCE/DETAIL SFX/TRANSITIONS/IMPACT buses and intentional ducking. Target approx −14 to −12 LUFS and peak ≤−1 dBTP where appropriate; **numbers don't replace listening on phones and headphones**.

**Historical integrity:** Claims, maps, numbers, quotes and documents need source lock. Credibly design originals and properly label reconstructions. Never create fake archives or assign an unsupported authentic identity to an AI illustration. License check all photo/footage and audio before commercial reuse. Rights for archived files are per asset, not site-level assumption.

**Release status:** Research → Story Locked → Hook Approved → Art Approved → Motion Animatic Approved → Audio Complete → RENDER CANDIDATE → Full Creative + Technical QA PASS → FINAL MASTER. DO NOT label a first encode FINAL.

## 3. EDITORIAL ENGINE: FIRST FRAME TO EARNED PAYOFF

First encoded frame MUST show the strange event already happening or a contradiction already legible—no logo, black-to-white fade, date card, empty background or generic “In 19XX…” opener.

**Hook process:**
1. Generate three DIFFERENT hook mechanisms (not three wording changes over the same shot).
2. For each, design exact first frame, 0–0.5s, 0.5–1s, 1–3s and 3–5s changes, first voice line, real physical/SFX cue, on-screen copy, unresolved question and source/accuracy risk.
3. At phone scale and muted, test: is the recognizable unusual event graspable in one glance? If not, REDESIGN, not just add text.
4. Use view-count or public successful-short examples for *hypotheses*, not claims about private retention. The only actual performance proof comes from user/platform analytics.
5. Select one hook only after the art is visually production-grade, not a rectangle placeholder. Reserve central caption safe zone from frame 1.

**Story rhythm:** consequence first → question → surprising complication → hidden mechanism → historical evidence → escalating consequences → earned answer. Use contrast, attention pivots and evidence, not generic every-2s artificial jump cuts. Define each 1–3s information change. Make every transition preserve a causal visual object, spatial axis or semantic link where useful; use hard cuts when a continuity wipe would be dishonest or distracting.

**Target runtime:** ~55–75s as a planning range, but do not force the natural voice into this slot. If a richer story needs 78s and earns all of it, quality and real audio timing outrank a rigid runtime target. Remove redundant lines rather than speaking unnaturally fast.

## 5. MINIMUM PREMIUM ART ASSET FLOOR — NO MORE OPENING-ONLY POLISH

No shot may fall below the approved opening's design care simply because it explains something technical. The middle of the film is often the most important mechanism; reserve adequate illustration time, original assets and layer rigging for it.

Every essential visible machine, building, car, environment, historical person or major prop MUST have:

1. **Recognizable period-correct silhouette** at phone-scale grayscale without a label.
2. **Subassembly hierarchy:** separately drawn working parts, pivots, direction of motion, plausible physical contacts, supports, constraints and occluders.
3. **Material language:** correct metal/wood/glass/paper/water/stone differentiation, purposeful line weights, believable joints, shading, and layer-specific textures.
4. **Spatial logic:** shadows/grounding and relative scale, perspective, near/mid/far planes, coherent camera motion and no floating objects.
5. **Documented sources:** maps/data/period details tied to factual references; asset rights/provenance/AI reconstruction disclosure when needed.
6. **Design anchors:** key objects carry local-space attachment points for labels, mask edges, animated arrows, evidence targets and exit match cuts.
7. **Detail for actual camera zoom:** no soft 900-pixel raster used as a closeup in a native 4K shot. Important background artwork cannot simply be a repeatedly stretched low-res image.
8. **Visual consistency:** full-height environmental layers and carefully modeled mechanics have comparable production values across all major shots, not a photo-real intro and toy-like vector people in the middle.

**Asset packet per hero subject:** `asset_id, exact history/source, component tree, rest pose, local anchor points, occlusion/mask data, pivot constraints, shading/material palette, textured closeup, phone silhouette preview, planned motion, rights status, owner, QA verdict`.

**Art preflight requires at least FIVE different demanding full-resolution style frames:** (1) hook consequence; (2) central mechanism/explanatory challenge; (3) authenticated archival or geographic evidence; (4) human-scale/physical environment; (5) climax/earned insight. They must belong to varied composition families. A frame that looks good ONLY because it uses an AI illustration, while its motion scene will fall back to stickmen, fails this preflight. If assets are not adequate, redesign before coding.

**Do not use generative images as fake documentary archive.** If original illustrated concepts are used, preserve provenance and avoid fabricated source maps, misleading letters, newspaper names or invented historical witnesses.

## 6. SCENE GRAPH, LAYERED CAMERA AND ACTUAL ANIMATION ENGINEERING

Each animated object exists in its **own local coordinates**, inherits independently controlled parent transforms and may carry linked masks/labels and animations. Cameras are separate timeline objects from scene content. Avoid `ctx.drawImage(sceneBackground)` plus globally scaling everything as a proxy for sophisticated 2.5D direction.

Minimum deterministic shot schema:

```javascript
// Conceptual schema, not a complete runnable renderer.
Episode {
  width:2160, height:3840, fps:30,
  voiceMaster, wordAlignment, soundCues, factualSources,
  shotLedger: [ Shot {
    id, claimId, narrationWordRange, timeStart, timeEnd,
    camera: { perspective, target, keyframes, easing },
    layers: [ Node {
      id, parentId, assetRef, zDepth, localTransformKeys,
      maskId, anchorPoints, grainSpace, material, sourceId
    }],
    annotations: [{parentAssetId, localCoordinates, revealTimes}],
    captions: {screenSpace:true, region:[0.42,0.58]},
    visualStateBeats, sfxLinks, nextShotMatchObject,
    compositionFingerprint, qaTests
  }]
}
```

2D parent transforms: `M_world(node,t)=M_world(parent,t) × M_local(node,t)`; camera then maps world to screen: `P(t) × V(t) × M_world(node,t)`. Preserve object/page-space local highlights through camera motion while narration captions remain screen-space overlays. Use z-aware parallax or authored 2.5D plane transforms; proper occlusion/masks, not identical affine movement across every layer.

Expose deterministic controls `setTime(seconds)`, `setFrame(frame)`, `renderFrame(frame)`, `getDuration()`, `getFPS()`; no dependence on accumulated `requestAnimationFrame` delta or live timer for exports. Browser-openable editor with Play/Pause/Replay/Scrub, event links, layer inspection, captions safe guide, and WebM export where supported. Primary MP4 comes from real FFmpeg-backed native frame pipeline, not a fake download button.

**Timeline derives from audio:** lock waveform onsets/offsets first. Assign camera, masks, structural movement, data reveals and Foley to claim/word cue IDs. Support easing diversity: step/hold, cubic ease, physically plausible slow mechanisms, restrained overshoot when justified, path stroke reveals, stagger and masking. Distinguish source object motion from camera travel by checking transformed anchors across adjacent frames.

**Pre-render motion evidence:** produce 2–5s moving **animatic micro-studies for the project's hardest shots** as production preflight when video production is explicitly authorized. These are original development previews, not copied tutorial sequences or an extra public film. Approval requires moving frames and UI-safe captions where relevant. If only a beautiful still is shown, the motion skill is NOT validated.

Preserve purposeful visual callback only if materially advancing the story.
