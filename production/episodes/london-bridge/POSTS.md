Why Is London Bridge in Arizona?

## YouTube description
London Bridge is in Arizona. And they rebuilt it on dry land before digging the channel beneath it.

In 1968, Robert McCulloch bought the old London Bridge for $2.46 million. Its numbered granite facing stones crossed the ocean, then traveled by truck to Lake Havasu City. A new reinforced concrete bridge carried the historic stonework. Crews built it on dry land before excavating the channel underneath.
