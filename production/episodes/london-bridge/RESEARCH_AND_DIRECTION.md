# London Bridge in Arizona

## Story lock
Question: how did a London landmark become the centerpiece of an Arizona development?
Thesis: McCulloch bought historic facing stones as an attraction; engineers rebuilt their appearance around a new concrete structure, constructed on dry land before a channel was excavated.

Sources accessed 13 October 2026:
- https://www.sundt.com/projects/london-bridge/ — participating contractor. Five-span reinforced concrete replacement, match-marked exterior granite, dry-land soffit-fill construction and later channel. Firsthand corporate retrospective; numbers not needed in narration.
