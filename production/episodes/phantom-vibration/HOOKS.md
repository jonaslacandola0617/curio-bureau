A / Recognition, selected from the owner-authorized premise: You felt your phone vibrate. You checked it. Nothing. So what did you actually feel? Image: pocket sensation annotation → inspection of the still device. Strength: immediate familiar experience. Risk: do not imply all absent notifications are phantom vibrations.

B / Contradiction: A buzz can feel real even when your phone never vibrated. Image: large stationary phone with felt/device states compared. Strength: compact thesis. Risk: gives away the explanation too soon.

C / Evidence: Researchers asked medical staff about a phone vibration that never happened. Image: original115/169 study summary then personal example. Strength: factual evidence. Risk: slower opening and a sample-specific statistic can be misread as a general rate.
