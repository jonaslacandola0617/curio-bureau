# Phantom vibration — full narration draft v1

You felt your phone vibrate. You checked it. Nothing. So what did you actually feel?

Sometimes, the phone never vibrated at all. That experience is called a phantom vibration.

One proposed explanation starts with what you expect.

When you're waiting for a call, your brain may interpret another sensation as the familiar buzz.

Pressure from clothing or a small muscle movement are possible candidates.

But researchers haven't pinned down exactly what causes it. Surveys document the experience; they don't prove that mechanism.

So a convincing buzz isn't always proof that your phone vibrated.

The sensation can feel real even when the alert wasn't.
