# Three hook mechanisms — proposals, not auditioned

## A — consequence first (selected for development)

- Frame zero: one small damaged site already visible on an illustrative probe frontal surface; clearly labeled illustration. No total ship explosion.
- 0–0.5s intention: orient to the crater; a dry, brief existing action cue marks the inspection, not a recording of sound traveling in vacuum.
- 0.5–1s: an independently registered grain magnifier identifies the tiny projectile on a different explicit scale.
- 1–3s: establish the relative speed and connect the grain to the damage. Keep center captions clear.
- 3–5s: pull out toward the larger encounter, opening the question of how such a small object can matter.
- Voice: N01, controlled surprise; score begins with a sparse plucked motif, then gains pulse on the speed reveal.
- Risk: invented crater dimensions/brightness; density/scale confusion; mitigate with qualitative illustration labels and separate scale windows.

## B — ordinary specimen becomes the encounter

- Frame zero: a very small grain in a clearly enlarged specimen view; a much larger reference pointer selects it without pretending to measure actual dust.
- 0–0.5s: settle the grain, tactile specimen/marker cue.
- 0.5–1s: replace specimen framing with the same grain’s encounter orientation.
- 1–3s: reveal a tiny hypothetical probe and the relative-speed label.
- 3–5s: cut to localized consequence on the frontal skin.
- Voice alternative: “A tiny grain can become a serious problem—if you hit it fast enough.”
- Risk: weaker sound-off first-frame stakes and a false sense that every grain guarantees destruction.

## C — apparent emptiness, then accumulated damage

- Frame zero: tiny probe in predominantly empty space; a close-up surface inset already shows several scars.
- 0–0.5s: register craft and inset, no generic starfield fly-through.
- 0.5–1s: select one scar; avoid random blinking particles.
- 1–3s: connect the apparently empty route to magnified material along it.
- 3–5s: resolve to one encounter, not an unexplained dense storm.
- Voice alternative: “The space between stars looks empty. A fast probe still has to survive it.”
- Risk: two competing eye targets and a weaker immediate single mechanism.

A best connects the first visible consequence to the causal explanation without repeating the prior Sun delay or Black Dwarf limit-fall reveals.
