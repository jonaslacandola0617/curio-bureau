# Research lock draft — relativistic dust

Checked 19 October 2026. Source-supported scope is deliberately narrower than a generic “dust destroys any starship” claim. No new direct Kurzgesagt video/animation analysis was performed in this step.

Access scope: Proposal context and dust/protective-coating challenge. Not a launch announcement or proof of viability; forum comments are not evidence.

## Optional reproducible calculation

Use KE=(gamma−1)mc²; gamma=1/sqrt(1−beta²). Assumed spherical grain diameter 1 micrometre; density 3,000 kg/m³; m=rho*pi*d³/6. At beta=0.2: m≈1.5708e−15 kg; gamma≈1.020620726; KE≈2.911 J. This is an illustrative chosen grain, not the typical grain or an observed deposit. Diameter is not radius. No explosive-equivalence analogy is needed. The spoken draft does not depend on this overlay.

## Exclusions and uncertainty

- No universal catastrophic collision, bomb analogy, continent/planet destruction or numerical survival probability.
- No nuclear reaction, chemical combustion, aerodynamic bow shock or audible environmental “wind” used as the dust mechanism.
- Do not turn total energy into an exact crater size without an independently supported coupling/material model.
- Do not visually equate larger grains used for explanation with typical encountered grains.
- Dust erosion and gas-particle damage are distinct; the episode develops dust, while acknowledging the medium also includes gas.
- Do not infer that travel is impossible, shielding is solved, dust density is uniform or mission hardware is finalized.
- No source-paper figure or photo is cleared simply because its paper can be read. Visual rights remain item-specific.

The question at the end is editorial synthesis, not a finding that any specific probe will survive.
