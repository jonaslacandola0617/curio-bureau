This prompt creates a qualitative art-direction preview only. It is not the required exact native three-state composition, source/rights lock, pinned-font render or owner-approved art. No diagram dimensions, data or source-paper text are generated. Generated previews are displayed in the conversation; no consuming code references a transient image path.

Create ONE preview-only art-direction contact sheet containing THREE equal tall 9:16 panels side by side, with slim ivory gutters and a quiet ivory outer margin. This is a Curio Bureau science-film CONCEPT, not a scientific diagram, not native final film frames, and no actual data is shown. Original sophisticated image-led editorial photographic illustration, navy #0D1C2B, ivory #F5F0E7, muted brass #C8A26B, restrained coral #D57864. Hero is the SAME small section of a hypothetical spacecraft's engineered graphite-like leading shield, shown as a convincing macro material photograph, rich specific layered print texture, controlled local photocopy/ink character, purposeful crop. Never a generic photo in a rectangular web card. Connect three states of the SAME surface and SAME marked contact point: LEFT BEFORE: a tiny irregular pale mineral grain approaches a pristine graphite surface diagonally; enough negative space and an intuitive magnified-grain inset make clear the tiny particle is enlarged for visibility, not a boulder. MIDDLE CONTACT: closer crop of that same contact point, a tightly localized bright ivory/coral flash and sparse material ejecta, intact surrounding shield clearly visible; no enormous fireball, nuclear imagery, flames, dramatic ship explosion or implied airflow; it is an explicitly qualitative impact illustration. RIGHT AFTER: that same shield orientation, now a small empty crater with crisp raised/ejected edges and convincing dark graphite texture; no continued glow, no dust storm, no destroyed craft. All three compositions should reserve the center band (42–58% of each panel) as naturally clean navy negative space for later Manrope captions, while keeping the explanatory surface and contact point in the lower third and grain/selection cue above center. Perspective and surface identity remain consistent. Give source-local imagery mild grain, fine photographic print detail and optical separation, not a global distressed haze. Hard precise image edges and clean contrast; NO diffuse text shadows, glow backdrops, blur behind labels, gradients behind type, or ornamental paper stacks. Put only tiny neutral uppercase labels 'BEFORE', 'CONTACT', 'AFTER' in the outer margin above the corresponding panels, plus an unobtrusive outer-margin footer 'CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION — NOT TO SCALE'. No equations, numbers, invented research excerpts, logos, source photos, copyrighted spacecraft concepts or fake scientific thermal maps. Three distinct causally connected states, polished subject-specific material art, mature documentary mood rather than cartoon or glossy sci-fi poster.
