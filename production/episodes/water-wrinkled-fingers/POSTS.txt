YOUTUBE SHORTS TITLE

Your Nerves Help Make These Wrinkles

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION

Why do your fingers wrinkle in water? It's an active response involving your nervous system and narrowing blood vessels, not simply passive soaking. A leading explanation links reduced volume beneath the skin to the folds above it.

The grip story is less settled. A 2013 study found faster handling of wet objects; a 2014 study did not reproduce that dexterity benefit. A 2021 study found improved grip-force efficiency in a different task. Those findings don't establish that wrinkles evolved for wet grip.
