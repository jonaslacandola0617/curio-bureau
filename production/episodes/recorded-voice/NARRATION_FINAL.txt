Why does your voice sound so different on a recording?
When you speak, you hear it through two routes.
Sound travels through the air to your ears.
But vibrations also travel through your skull and other tissues to your inner ears.
That extra route changes the sound you're used to.
A normal microphone captures the sound in the air, not those internal vibrations.
So playback leaves out part of your usual experience.
The microphone and room can change it, too.
Both versions are you. You're hearing them through different routes.
