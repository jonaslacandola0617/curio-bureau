YOUTUBE SHORTS — TITLE

Why Your Recorded Voice Sounds Different 🎙️ #Shorts

YOUTUBE SHORTS — COPY-READY DESCRIPTION

Your voice reaches your ears through two routes when you speak: sound travelling through the air, and vibrations travelling through your skull and other tissues.

A normal microphone captures the airborne sound. Playback leaves out part of the experience you're used to—and the microphone and room can change the sound, too.

Both versions are you. You're hearing them through different routes.

Does hearing your recorded voice still surprise you?
