Title: Why Astronauts Get Taller in Space 🚀 #Shorts

Space can make you taller—but you don’t get to keep the extra height.

Astronauts can gain up to 3% in height in orbit. Most of the change happens in the spine, as its normal loading is reduced in microgravity. Back on Earth, their normal height returns.
