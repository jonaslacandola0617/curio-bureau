Sources used in this film:
• USACE preliminary analysis of the February2025 Cumberland River Basin floods: https://www.dvidshub.net/news/492605/corps-engineers-projects-prevent-estimated-734-million-flood-damages-during-february-storms
• Published USACE comparison map: https://www.dvidshub.net/image/8910491/corps-engineers-projects-prevent-estimated-734-million-flood-damages-during-february-storms
• JICA FY2023 ex-post evaluation, including the Imus record: https://www2.jica.go.jp/en/evaluation/pdf/2023_PH-P253_4_f.pdf

The US comparison includes an estimated without-project scenario. The Philippine evaluation reports improvement through interviews while comparable flooded-area and household measurements were unavailable; its overall evaluation was satisfactory. These are separate projects and types of evidence. Missing data does not mean zero benefit.
