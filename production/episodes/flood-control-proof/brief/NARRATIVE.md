# Flood-control proof — narration draft v1

Selected episode. Draft; not recorded or caption-aligned.

You can photograph a flood wall. But not the flood it prevented.

Engineers tackle that missing comparison with models.

Run the same storm with the project—and without it. Keep the exposed homes and other starting conditions comparable.

A dry street after a weaker storm isn't the same test.

Compare water depth, flooded area and the people still at risk.

But a model needs a reality check: measured water levels, flood records, and tests against other storms.

A Philippine evaluation shows why that matters. At Imus, interviews reported improvement, but comparable flooded-area and household data were unavailable.

Those are different kinds of evidence.

And protection has limits. Maintenance matters. Bigger floods can exceed the design.

So don't stop at “Was something built?” Ask: “How much risk was reduced, for whom—and how do you know?”
