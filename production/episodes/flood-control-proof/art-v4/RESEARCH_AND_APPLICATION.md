## Construction grammar applied here

A scene begins with a proposition and a useful object relationship. Crop, scale and overlap establish the dominant source; secondary material supports that object. A small diagram can be valid when it stays physically or evidentially connected to its subject. A new representation requires an orienting label and a reason to change modes. The stable coordinate system is part of the explanation, not merely a convenient renderer implementation.

Motion is assigned to the part that changes meaning. Independent masks, component layers, source-local coordinates and occluders are plausible rebuild architectures inferred from observed pictures; they are not recovered Vox source files. Labels and captions remain separate from photographic optics. Texture should differ between water, masonry, print and film. A grain layer does not repair an incoherent spatial model.
