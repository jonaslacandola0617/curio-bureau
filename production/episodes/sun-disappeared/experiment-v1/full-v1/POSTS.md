# YouTube Shorts

Title: If the Sun Disappeared, When Would You Notice? ☀️

If the Sun vanished, daylight wouldn’t end instantly. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth—and, in this impossible thought experiment, Earth’s orbit wouldn’t change immediately either.

The Sun cannot actually vanish. This is a conceptual explanation of propagation delay, not a physically possible event or a disaster simulation. The orbit diagram simplifies the Sun–Earth system and is not to scale.
