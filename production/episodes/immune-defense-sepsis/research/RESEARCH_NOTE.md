# Case choice and evidence boundaries

The active broad question is narrowed to sepsis as an infection-related dysregulated host response with organ dysfunction. Vessel leakage/clotting/perfusion are selected as visible pathways, not a complete model. This is not a generic autoimmune diagnosis and not an instruction to suppress immunity. Do not label all inflammatory responses harmful or all infections sepsis.

NIGMS education contains broad older wording about noninfectious insults. Use the infection-linked WHO/CDC/Sepsis-3 framing in the script. NIGMS remains a source for basic vascular and immune-suppression context, not a reason to silently redefine sepsis. The2026 procalcitonin paper primarily uses mice and endothelial-cell systems; its treatment finding must not become a claim of established human therapy. The script contains no prevalence, mortality or outcome numbers.

The specimen micrographs are actual NIH/NIAID phagocytosis records. They are not patient sepsis images, measured blood flow, natural visible colors or time-lapse. Hand art is a generated illustrative photographic asset; no injury, diagnosis or clinical observation is inferred. Models are original conceptual, not-to-scale illustrations with assigned materials and no measured timeline. Gold denotes fluid tracer in the barrier view and separately labeled oxygen-delivery symbols in the tissue view; clarify the legend at each representation change.

No fresh audience dashboard review, trend measurement, outcome test or sound audition was performed. Accessible question framing is a creative hypothesis. The old six-post local/universal experiment is not commissioned; the owner has now commissioned these three explicit science questions.
