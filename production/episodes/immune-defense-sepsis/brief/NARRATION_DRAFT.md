Could your body's defense system hurt you?

Your immune system normally helps fight infections. But in sepsis, that response becomes dangerously dysregulated.

Look at the lining of a tiny blood vessel.

Inflammatory signals can make this barrier leaky. Fluid moves into surrounding tissue. At the same time, clotting can obstruct small vessels.

That can disrupt blood flow, so organs struggle to get the oxygen they need.

The infection matters. But part of the damage comes from the body's response to it.

Sepsis is more complicated than an immune system simply being too strong. Some defenses can also become suppressed.

It is a medical emergency. Early treatment matters.

Protection depends on control—not just force.
