## YouTube Shorts

Title: The Dead Star That Could Still Explode

Description:
A dead star. A falling limit. One more possible ending.

A theoretical black-dwarf model explores how an extremely slow change in composition could make some massive stellar remnants collapse in the far future. The earliest estimate is roughly 10^1100 years—if protons do not decay first.

This is a hypothesis, not an observed explosion or a prediction for our Sun. Black-dwarf imagery and internal diagrams are illustrative.

Research: M. E. Caplan, Black Dwarf Supernova in the Far Future (2020):
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02296
