## Four original visual briefs

| Board | Visual argument and alternatives | Chosen construction | Future information state / layer needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Voice | A microphone captures the airborne route; hearing your own voice includes air and transmission through the body. Considered two equal photo cards versus an unequal ear/microphone collage. | Organic monochrome ear crop against an orange division; smaller photographic microphone strip; yellow route selection. This emphasizes recognition and distinction rather than pretending to show internal anatomy. | Ear print, microphone print, background division, route labels and annotation are independent. A later source-backed skull/model detail would be needed to explain conduction; no such motion is implemented. |
| 02 — Microgravity | Spinal unloading can temporarily increase stature. Considered a false before/after astronaut height comparison versus a context photograph beside a labeled physical spine model. | The model provides subject detail; a much larger NASA astronaut and Earth establish microgravity context. Scale is editorial, not a measured comparison. | Backing/model/astronaut/Earth/type/annotation separate. Later inspection must preserve model identity; never depict bones growing or claim the astronaut image measures height. |
| 03 — Light | Visible white light contains wavelengths that a prism separates. Considered a generic rainbow drawing versus a real photograph of the optical object and spectral colours. | Evidence-dominant photographed prism in a dark plastic/print field; type wraps above/below the object. Actual spectral colours remain unchanged. | Print/photo/type separate. Later zoom should inspect a real spectrum; the low-resolution source is not cleared for large native-4K close-ups. Find a stronger source or reduce footprint first. |
| 04 — Vision | The brain supplies visual continuity at the blind spot. Considered a misleading iris hole versus a missing edge strip of a photographic print. | Full-width monochrome eye above the proposition; an absent strip at the photograph’s edge is explicitly metaphorical. Iris intact. | Photo/print gap/material/headline separate. Later demonstrate an accurate fixation-card experiment or retinal source; the metaphor alone does not establish anatomical location. |

## Scientific support

- Voice: [Orepic et al., own-voice research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9929504/) and [Hosaka, Kimura and Yotsumoto](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-80095-6). Mic/room response can also affect playback; the board simplifies to the main route distinction and is not a complete claim that all differences have one cause.
- Microgravity: [NASA spinal ultrasound investigation](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/spinal-ultrasound-investigation/) and [ESA Mission X space-height activity](https://missionx.esa.int/whats-your-space-height/). Temporary spinal change is not bone growth. These prior-episode sources were retained with the actual model/photograph notices.
- Light: [NASA visible light explanation](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/09_visiblelight/). The photograph shows a real glass prism and reflected/refracted spectral patches, not a calibrated wavelength chart or a single isolated output ray.
- Vision: [Exploratorium blind-spot explanation](https://www.exploratorium.edu/snacks/blind-spot). The optic-disc region lacks photoreceptors; perceptual filling-in depends on surrounding information. [NEI eye pathway](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/healthy-vision/how-eyes-work) supports the retina/optic-nerve distinction.

## Checks and limits

The HTML embeds assets/fonts and makes no remote requests. Four selectable compositions use independent DOM image, material, type and annotation layers. Browser checks passed for both font families, every image and zero page errors. All four rendered boards were visually inspected; the ear-route text contrast and the light board’s crowded bottom type were corrected. This is internal inspection, not owner approval.

The supplied originals remain in the private source bundle. Preview copies preserve aspect ratio and use WebP compression with a 2400-pixel bound. Prism is 950 pixels wide and eye is 1463 pixels wide; large native-4K crops would exceed their source coverage. Static review previews are not final 4K visual certification. Film jitter, focus hunt, motion blur, temporal grain, edge boil, parallax, SFX, voice, captions and video export are **not tested or implemented here**.

Future-caption guides are available, but no phrase-specific caption or platform-control test passed. Board 02 and 04 especially require staged reframing before voiced motion; captions must not cover the model, astronaut face or iris. Bottom explanatory labels must move to a safe area or into narration in an upload version.

All four boards: **OWNER REVIEW PENDING**. Review subject choice, metaphor clarity, crop/integration, composition, type and material separately. Approval of one board does not approve the other three, all palettes, an entire future episode or a completed moving sequence.
