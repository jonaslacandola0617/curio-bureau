# Curio Bureau — finished static visual tests

**Revision 2 · Owner review pending · 15 October 2026 Asia/Manila.** Four 1080×1920 PNGs and a self-contained HTML gallery. The gallery has a Finishing on/off comparison and the existing caption guide. This is a finished still-treatment test, not approval of the four artworks or an animated film.

## What was implemented

- Stronger existing paper, fibre, photocopy and plastic materials, differentiated by object role. Existing material opacity increased 1.35×, bounded at 0.8.
- Photograph-local print dots: 4 logical-pixel spacing, lower opacity on the real coloured prism evidence. Source-alpha masks keep cutout print treatment off unrelated backgrounds.
- Chromatic aberration on the photographic periphery: separate red and green/blue channels offset +2.8/−2.4 logical pixels; central evidence remains unshifted. The prism uses reduced strength. Headlines and functional labels keep their original colours.
- Compound focus: a registered blurred copy masked into the image periphery; 2.6px for most photographic edges, 4px at the eye’s outer context, 4.5px on the distant Earth. Sharp central detail is retained. Earth is circularly masked to remove the source photograph’s black rectangular surround.
- A static optical light/dark falloff, photographic grain, sparse dust and complementary print texture. Grain is anchored to each board. This is one frozen exposure, not exposure animation.
- Local ink treatment: 0.8px contour irregularity and 0.18px diffusion on editorial headlines/annotations; printed labels receive a subtle material shadow. This is a static ink-edge state, not animated edge boil.
- Full-resolution PNG export from the browser at 1080×1920, rather than screenshots of the small gallery cards.

These are original browser implementations of the previously discussed treatments, not recovered Vox proprietary settings. The composition, sources, credit and factual qualifications from the original source audit remain applicable. No source artwork from the creator portfolios is included.

## What the stills cannot establish

Mechanical jitter, brightness shifts, grain cadence, edge boil, focus hunts, text choreography, decisive parallax, motion blur, synchronized SFX and music require an actual moving/audio test. Their contracts remain in the existing infrastructure; they are not falsely marked implemented by a PNG. The next stage is the owner-approved motion integration test.

The caption guide is still a layout aid. No word-specific or platform-control caption review has passed. Scientific model/composite/metaphor labels remain visible. The prism and eye source-resolution limits from the original audit still apply to larger native-4K close-ups.
