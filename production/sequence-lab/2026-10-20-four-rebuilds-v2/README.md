## Craft and delivery

Each is exactly360 native1080×1920 frames/12seconds at30fps. Scene/camera/material time is quantized to12fps; natural voice and the shared unmodified30fps London Bridge ending use actual time. Photographic source detail supplies physical texture; deterministic moving soft-light grain overlays and brief source-image focus hunts settle before the next reading state. Labels/captions use clean contrast and crisp supports, without blurred shadows. Fonts are pinned Playfair Display900 and Manrope800; captions sit near vertical center. Diomede's folio contrast was corrected for its new dark field.

The unchanged natural Bernard/Clyde recordings retain their exact legacy ID and full decoded tails. Every topic has a newly keyed original six-note plucked score with evolving sections, decaying overtones, stereo room taps and voice ducking. Existing verified original SFX follow specific actions. Cue identity, timings, source hashes, normalization and full source lengths are recorded in per-topic `AUDIO_RECEIPT.json`. Sounds are synthesized editorial illustrations, not authentic recordings of the mechanisms. Machine audio checks are not independent listening approval. Caption timings are inherited actual-audio phrase timings; they are not newly forced-aligned.

Source is frozen before native capture. Each closed120-frame lossless RGB chunk is uploaded immediately to its own versioned GitHub Release. Each final H.264 High/yuv420p MP4 with AAC48kHz stereo320kbps is uploaded as soon as it closes, before extended review. All older releases remain intact. `SOURCE_LOCK.json` hashes critical inputs; `READY.json` binds the lock. The build verifies every hash. Public downloads require separate final inspection.
