## Chosen passage

**Proposition:** a normal microphone captures the sound in the air, rather than the speaker's original internally transmitted self-voice component. Microphone and room response can also alter the recording; the full explanation retains that qualification.

**Initial:** an original speaking-portrait cutout faces the actual microphone. A gold external sound field and a coral body-associated overlay distinguish the two categories. These are explicitly conceptual, not measured waves or anatomy.

**Intermediate:** the airborne selection is being extracted into a physical recording strip. The speaker, microphone and original body-associated layer persist. The captured gold fragment and the source pattern must match; paper is the record surface, not a universal photo frame.

**Resolved:** the extracted strip contains the external category and the existing approved narration's signal envelope. The internal category stays with the speaker. The result makes the selection legible without adding a new flowchart or relying on a headline to say what happened.

The original portrait is an illustration, not a photograph of the actual narrator or an experiment participant. No generated anatomical structures will be used. The recording envelope is a visualization of the actual approved narration, not a comparison of conduction amplitudes or an experiment measuring internal transmission.
