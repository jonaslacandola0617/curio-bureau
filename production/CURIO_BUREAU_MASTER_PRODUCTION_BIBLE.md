Distinct documentary, physical-object, technical, map, quantitative and spatial-archive representations do not need one universal beige-paper look. Classify video regions **G = authored full-frame graphics**, **O = authored overlay over filmed footage**, **C = mixed composite: graphics components only**, **R = real video/interviews/archival motion: excluded**, **P = photograph/history image/base map pixels: excluded**, **X = uncertain: explicitly qualify**. ### Accepted original graphic behavior contracts (each must serve a documented claim)
1. **Reveal → comprehension → next consequence.** Animate the smallest variable that teaches the current sourced fact, then deliberately hold a dense graphic long enough to read before its next labelled interpretation. V02 94–100s and V04 123–130s demonstrate purposeful graphic holds; **stillness when informative is allowed and expected**. Motion solely to prevent the screen looking static is a failure.
2. **Data and meaning are independent layers.** For ordinal grids/maps, first establish a stable authored baseline, animate **data-accurate** membership/fill as separately named states, reveal the correct threshold or interpretation after the evidence settles, and preserve the resolved graphic for reading. Observed V02 141–148s, V01 72–78s; tested in original rig **01 information matrix**. An arbitrary looping number counter without source truth is forbidden.
3. **Causal connections are spatial obligations, not decorative arrows.** Introduce the source node and destination before drawing a relationship. A link grows along an owned path between **source-local** anchors, changes only when the story's mechanism advances, and the endpoint's meaning remains legible. V01 112–118s; original rig **05 causal path**.
4. **Three coordinate systems and real occlusion are required where needed.** Every significant graphic can specify its source/world-local transform, independent virtual camera transform, and final caption/screen coordinate transform. `screenAnchor(t) = viewport × camera(t) × parentGraphic(t) × localGraphic(t) × sourcePoint`; source-attached arrows, highlights and map labels must follow the same parent. Occluder masks are independently named/z-sorted; do not disguise clipping bugs as blur. Captions remain screen-fixed around 42–58% height. V03 57–64s, V04 22–30s and 525–533s; original rigs **02 tracked neutral shape** and **08 layered map anchors**. For regression tests with known synthetic anchors target **≤1 screen pixel alignment error**.
5. **Evidence must remain identifiable through animation.** Display an original/cleared source quote, object or map first, then place a highlight, symbol or evidence annotation with a **source-relative anchor** to the exact proposition/feature. V02 445–452s; original rig **07 invented memo highlight**. Do not fabricate source-document sentences or reuse source Vox filmed/still imagery.
6. **Physical scales and mechanisms decompose into real components.** A mechanical/meter explanation needs actual independently moving material parts, contacts/hinges, tick geometry, masks and separately timed numeric meaning. V03 143–151s shows a designed ball/airflow illustrative system (not validated simulation of aerodynamics); V05 108–116s shows staged thermometer fill and delayed number. Original rigs **03 scale**, **04 multicomponent crank**. A circle or rectangle tween is not a substitute for an authentic period-specific apparatus in a finished film.
7. **Graphic semantic transitions must be honest.** When converting explanatory modes (chart→map, statistic→mechanism, document→route), only morph if geometries, data domains and causal meaning truly correspond. Otherwise an intentional edited **cut** with visual continuity is preferable to a visually clever but false topology. V05 244–252s; original rig **06 graph to mechanism**. The reference shows a representation change, **not proven continuous vector morph**.
8. **Maps are spatial evidence, not reusable illustrations.** Use separately sourced geographic/era-accurate coordinates or label a generic map as invented. Camera zoom must preserve named region labels and evidence markers in source coordinates, with event reveal after viewers are oriented (V04 22–30s, 525–533s). Never adopt Vox's original historical map pixels or implied geopolitical positions as Curio-verified primary facts.
9. **Phone-first composition and anti-template editing.** Keep hero visual data, highlights and dense labels clear of center-safe Manrope captions and platform controls. The six-shot fingerprint must record `representation_family, narrative_fact, source_parent, camera_motion, graphic_state_change, held_read_time, occluder, transition_reason`. A repeated source with changed camera/scale is legitimate only if it explains a new causal state; repeated ornamental headline/cards are not.
10. **Moving preflight remains mandatory.** For each difficult graphic build a seven-state 0/25/50/75/100 storyboard plus onset/overshoot/hold where needed; store absolute-frame deterministic video samples, inspect first/middle/end at phone scale, and test source anchor stability, mask/occlusion, readable data values and frame-complete motion. Eight original v2 rig clips validate the *engineering classes*, not final film quality. New episodes still need five premium styleframes, film-specific challenging motion previews and subjective beginning-middle/end QA.

**Status:** APPROVED channel-wide improvement to the v1.1 Bible. User explicitly authorized adoption of the October 2026 Motion Mastery Lab. ### Evidence limits, and why they matter
Nine user-provided MP4s were verified and decoded in the Motion Mastery Lab. They were predominantly **third-party Vox-inspired tutorial and demonstration edits**, not authenticated originals of Vox documentaries. The study maps 85 approximate visual/edit beats, 35 densely sampled motion windows and 35 transferable animation techniques. The study decoded/measured audio but did not complete a human perceptual soundtrack audition. Full official Vox productions and private watch-retention metrics remain separate evidence gaps. Never claim mastery or Vox authorship from these tutorials alone. No Vox logos, bespoke graphics, source audio or protected film clips enter original Curio assets or public GitHub storage.

### P0+ art-quality continuity: the weakest explanatory shot matters
Before code/rendering, approve **five genuinely different, difficult, production-quality style frames**: opening consequence, principal physical/causal mechanism, evidence/map/source treatment, human/environment shot and closing implication. Critical middle scenes MUST achieve approximately the same designed-asset and composition quality as the opening. Fail designs with a painted hero opening but stick-figure workers, rectangular period buildings, generic mechanical blocks or toy-like mid-film schemas. Key objects require period-correct **recognizable silhouettes, multi-part functional construction, pivots/contact points, coherent ground shadows/material cues, depth/occlusion planes, camera/annotation anchors, and verified evidence**. A standalone title/diagram rectangle is not a substitute for a constructed explanatory system.

### Layered-camera and source-coordinate architecture
Use independent object and camera transforms inside a deterministic, absolute-time scene graph. Every significant source (document, photo, map or machine) has a parent transform; highlights, arrows and labels intended to follow a source exist in **that source's local coordinates**. Captions stay independently in 42–58% **screen-space center-safe region**. Implement/verify multi-plane parallax, masking/occlusion, anchor points, source texture position, raster pixel density at maximum zoom and proper font loading. A universal CSS zoom of one flattened scene is not equivalent to camera-directed parallax. When possible maintain continuity between object contours/locations across shot boundaries.

### Motion-X-ray proof BEFORE committing to the full render
For each hero explanation and each unusually difficult motion technique, store a storyboard **motion X-ray**: 7+ representative time samples, estimated object/camera/annotation paths, information state at 0/25/50/75/100%, first/last composition and source/audio cue. Mark observed reference vs inferred rig, and cite the tutorial clip-ID/time where helpful. During *authorized film production*, validate the hardest techniques with short, ORIGINAL moving preflight clips or a moving animatic (e.g. 2–5 seconds): actual motion, depth, masks, timing and reading at phone scale must pass—not just a beautiful still image. Separate motion exercises remain optional if the user did not authorize tests and are never compulsory as copied Vox reconstructions.

### Non-template identity and attention choreography
Require a shot-fingerprint ledger: `composition family / intended eye path / source/evidence / depth planes / camera travel / object state change / local mask / matched exit anchor / palette role / audio event / caption footprint / craft verdict`. Inspect sliding **six-shot windows** and compare preceding three episodes. Near-identical adjacent scenes differing only by text are a red flag; three similar camera+composition fingerprints in six shots must be editorially justified or redesigned. Stable Curio identity is in Playfair+Manrope, navy/ivory/gold/coral role, evidence honesty, precise sound and high-detail art; flexible creative direction is in topic-specific environments, explanatory mechanisms, shot layout, camera, accents, timbral scoring and duration.

### Evidence-led editing rather than decorative motion
Build each key explanation through `claim → source/object → eye-path focus → functional movement/reveal → new state/evidence → semantic exit`. A camera push MUST reveal a new fact, object relationship, source detail or narrative implication. Motions are timed to real narration words and story events; don't force a new transition every second or reuse the same rough circle, document zoom or “Vox yellow” treatment. Reference-based suggested action times are creative starting points, not an invariant Vox formula.

### Additional required review artefacts
- `asset_packets/`: component graph, era/provenance, anchors, materials, licensed rights, phone-scale silhouette and close-up check.
- `shot_fingerprint.csv` and `motion_xrays/`: anti-repetition and evidence of internal movement.

# PART I — AUTHORITATIVE GOVERNING RULES

# 13 — CURIO BUREAU MASTER CREATIVE, MOTION, AUDIO & PRODUCTION BIBLE
**Authority:** This is the compact, enforceable **single source of truth** for Curio Bureau after its seven-episode initial review. Detailed rationale and diagrams live in 01–12. If an older episode prompt conflicts with this Bible's October 2026 safeguards, this document takes precedence unless the user explicitly approves a revised standard.

## Section A — Fixed identity
**Channel:** Curio Bureau. **Promise:** Stories, systems, and surprises. **Mission:** Reveal the hidden decisions and physical systems behind genuine surprising events with high documentary credibility and cinematic 2D/2.5D visual explanation. Voice is curious, confident, concise, ethically restrained.

**Palette core:** navy `#0D1C2B`, ivory `#F5F0E7`, gold `#C8A26B`, coral `#D57864`. Navy/ivory form hierarchy, gold means evidence/important connection, coral means changing/danger state. Episode-subject colors allowed for world/material; do not reproduce Vox yellow-on-black branding.

**Fonts:** Playfair Display for major editorial statements and final revelation, Manrope for labels, data, captions and source info. Pin OFL font files internally, verify families, hashes and actual raster specimens, do not rely on visual resemblance or network load. Primary title 76–108 logical px, captions 42–55 logical px, support labels 18–36 logical px based on role.

**Folio:** restrained C/B mark and source/location markers, not a permanent header plus large headline plus bottom dark caption bar. **Closing:** final story geometry merges into concise Curio Bureau mark; 0.8–1.8s branded tail.

**Caption discipline:** narration phrase text centered in normalized vertical band 42–58% with dynamic in-band placement; scenes are composed AROUND its footprint. Must survive TikTok and YouTube Shorts UI masks. Actual word alignment required for FINAL; estimated captions = WIP.

## Section B — Flexible filmmaking vocabulary
**Shot families:** full-bleed physical consequence, section/exploded mechanism, object macro, human scale, photo archive, accurate map, data comparison, source-document macro, calibrated before/after, asymmetric collage, POV, chronological construction, typographic argument, environmental payoff. See 07.

**Motion choice by cause:** physical events follow physically justified timing (friction, water, turning, gravity), maps/camera use clear eased interpolation, photos use subtle cinematic refocus plus source detail, headlines track narrator emphasis. Each scene answers what changed and why. Semantic shape match preferred to generic fade, but never fake geometry just to create a morph.

**Art direction:** subject-specific environments must have recognizable detailed multi-part silhouettes: 1919 streets with period rails/carts, 1967 period cars/junctions, Chicago masonry/ratchet beams, genuine lake/mine geometry. No stick figures or undetailed block facades as major narrative objects. Premium raster illustration without matching-quality vector mechanisms is inconsistent and fails director review.

**Avoid sameness:** no >2 consecutive equivalent headline/layout/camera shots unless visibly progressive causal animation; maintain shot fingerprint ledger and test 6 consecutive compositions. Every episode needs its own unique hero physical motion and different subject-correct environment palette; do not merely recolor prior animations.

## Section C — Story architecture
1. First visible frame already contains strange action. NO black, logo, date-card pre-roll.
2. By 3s viewers understand the weird premise; by 5s one strong causal question exists.
3. One central question, 5–7 logically escalating beats: consequence → context → hidden mechanism → human/consequence → evidence → changed interpretation.
4. Research and material rights locked before full script.
5. Three materially distinct hooks with phone-mute comprehension preflight; choose by accuracy, clarity, intrigue and production feasibility.
6. No forced dramatic claims; accurate calendar/government rules, mechanics, human casualties and dates are essential.
7. Final explanation changes the meaning of first frame; integrate a short brand tag with no dead-air tail.

## Section D — Narration, sound and caption hard gates
**VOICE drives all cut points.** Record natural voice first, decode, trim genuine boundaries and lock one WAV as timing authority. Normal transition 0.12–0.55s; unintended >0.85s is editorial FAIL; >1.20s between voice passages is absolute release FAIL. Measure within clips, not only between clips. Never fill 8–14s fixed visual windows with 3–5s clips. No eSpeak, forced unnatural tempo, mispronounced names or missing final word. Final voice tail ≤1.8s normally.

**SOUND:** separate VOICE/MUSIC/AMBIENCE/TRANSITIONS/DETAIL/IMPACTS buses, original/cleared real instrumentation with perceptible melodic structure and physically synchronized SFX, critically listen on phone and headphones. Target -14 to -12 LUFS integrated and ≤-1 dBTP true peak, with 48k stereo. LUFS is necessary technical data, not creative approval. Maintain asset licenses.

**CAPTIONS:** Manrope, 1–2 lines central band, actual final voice word-aligned and checked with platform masks on final MP4 and WebM. Never leave a missing `WordBoundary` unreported or call syllable-estimated timing final.

## Section E — Image and archival integrity
Only cite historical images actually cleared for the intended use. Never generate fake archival photographs or newspapers with invented words. Author original simulations as reconstruction (and label when ambiguity possible). Maps use credible physical locations; numeric claim uses source and context. Graphs/sections cannot invent exact stress, flow or scale values.

## Section F — Mandatory two-format native 4K delivery
Primary authoritative video: **2160×3840 30fps H.264 MP4** encoded with high-quality native vector/PDF/SVG/Canvas frames, AAC ≥~320kbps stereo 48k. Second authoritative file: **2160×3840 30fps VP9 WebM**, Opus 256–320kbps stereo 48k. Burn captions into BOTH; also supply final SRT, original interactive HTML studio and research/QA/stems as project files.

Native-4K PNG sequence is the pipeline gold standard: each 2160×3840 frame rendered individually from high-resolution originals, **not an upscaled 1080p movie**. High-quality JPEG transfer and pixel-upscaled art should be recorded as deviations and cannot pass strict gold-standard rendering QA. Verify actual pixel quality at 100% in both encodes, not only ffprobe width.

## Section G — Twelve gate production process
(1) source inventory → (2) fact lock → (3) 3 hooks/5s animatics → (4) final natural voice and word timings → (5) storyboard+shot fingerprints → (6) 5 full-quality style frames → (7) complete lower-resolution timed animatic → (8) subject-specific assets/license check → (9) full motion + stems/mix → (10) 1080p creative PRE-4K review → (11) lossless native 4K MP4/WebM → (12) full encoded/pixel/audio/phone UI QA.

**P0 release fail:** unsupported fact, unlicensed crucial archive, truncated media, wrong 4K resolution, missing 48k stereo, broken captions, app UI occlusion, >1.2s voice gap, absent music/voice/effects, illegible type, final-font fallback. **P1 creative fail:** generic placeholder graphics, repeated empty cards, bland static first frame, huge illustration-quality discontinuity, no meaningful animated mechanism, repetitive shot compositions, score feels like inaudible tone, uninspiring payoff. **P2 polish:** fine-grain consistency, kerning, historical footnotes and minor texture distinctions.

Final status can ONLY be 'FINAL MASTER' after creator reviews **entire actual final MP4 and WebM at normal speed**, checks actual voice stem, knows rights and verifies technical + creative gates. Source code running or FFmpeg success by itself is insufficient.

## Section H — Research and retention integrity
Public competitor view counts ≠ retention. The archive only contains first-party Curio video files, not channel audience retention curves. # 07 — MOTION & ANIMATION BIBLE | Curio Bureau v1
## The contract
Movement is documentary explanation. Every motion action must answer **WHAT changed? WHY? IN WHICH DIRECTION? HOW MUCH?** A sophisticated but uninformative zoom or fade fails. An animation lasting seven seconds must contain a *sequence of evolving knowledge*, not a beautiful initial diagram and six seconds of holding.

## 1. Basic animation grammar
**Motion channels:** `{scene.camera, scene.world, scene.hero, scene.mechanism, scene.evidence, scene.annotation, scene.caption, scene.audio}`. Each is separately timeline-controlled and composited. No scene may move a camera purely to avoid stagnation without a new causal reveal.

**Easing presets (not mandatory for physical motion):**
- Fast reveal / cursor highlight: cubic ease-out `1 − (1−t)^3` (fast settle), 0.18–0.38s.
- Polished camera reframe: cubic ease-in-out `3t² − 2t³`, 0.45–1.1s.
- Historical photo drift: smoothed long camera 1.5–4s plus new evidence overlay within first 1s; drift ALONE never fills the beat.
- Physical cause: use event-specific acceleration (fall gravity, winch rotation, water/viscosity, driver braking, ratchet step). Do not apply same generic cubic to all matter.
- Sudden consequence: sharp 0.08–0.18s anticipation/impact into physically slower recovery; sound hit must connect.
- Headline conclusion: 0.25–0.55s mask/reveal and hold only long enough to absorb claim; end card ≤1.8s after spoken last word.

**Virtual camera:** define the film with at least THREE distinct shot scales (macro, human/object, systemic map/section). Use a 2.5D stack of 4–7 meaningful planes: environment backdrop (0.08× parallax), buildings (0.25×), scene props (0.55×), hero object (1.0×), evidence layer (1.05×), caption/folio fixed screen-space. Recalibrate per story; never move labels with the distant scenery if unreadable.

**Continuity:** match one real causal line/object between shots—shoreline → contour line → mine tunnel; pressure seam → flood wave; road centerline → H sign; screw threads → engineering elevation. If match impossible, use an intentional hard editorial cut rather than template fade.

**Transitions:** at most one generic dissolve in any rolling 15 seconds unless source material demands it; semantic match cuts are preferred, but do not force a match where it misstates physics. Sound triggers are scarce meaningful actions, not every scene change.

## 2. Fourteen composition families — with function, assets and motion
| ID | Family | Causal job | Required assets | Correct dynamic behavior | QA mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Consequence full-bleed | Show the unbelievable event before context | detailed cinematic environment + event elements | event already developing frame 1 | headline first, event later |
| C02 | Physical mechanism sectional | Reveal hidden path/load/pressure | cross-section with named parts and load arrows | introduce one mechanism part every narration clause | generic slab rectangles |
| C03 | Object macro | Connect small cause to big effect | high-detail screw/rivet/pipe/cart component | real rotation/strain/change | basic icon tween |
| C04 | Human scale | Explain risk or coordination | designed period figures/real sources, believable scale | gesture→physical reaction→new stake | stickmen and speculative witness speech |
| C05 | Archival proof | Ground claim in primary media | cleared high-resolution photo/print/document | reframe, highlight exact cited detail; cut to meaning | archival used as wallpaper |
| C06 | Geographic system | Establish causality in space | sourced GIS/map geometry | region→point→direction→physical consequence | arbitrary country/lake blob |
| C07 | Data spatial comparison | Answer how many/how much | data visual semantic scales | numeric change plus visible physical relation | solitary giant stat on navy |
| C08 | Documentary paper macro | Highlight evidence conclusion | verified scan with item-level rights | 1 original phrase highlighted timed to voice | generated fake newspaper |
| C09 | Temporally locked before/after | Show change while geography is stable | same camera/map/section both states | matched wipe aligned to physical landmark | unrelated split cards |
| C10 | Asymmetric collage | Unify independent source types | clipped original photo, plan, annotation, map | source A docks into mechanism B | repeated scrapbook template |
| C11 | First-person explanatory camera | Make difficult physical system intuitive | authored POV with real props | track through machine / along pipe / inside vehicle | fake historic POV presented as record |
| C12 | Timeline across cause | Distinguish multi-year events | source-dated labels + actual places | each date changes depicted system | dates on static card with no context |
| C13 | Kinetic editorial argument | One short paradox statement | verified phrase, key object/mask | words uncover physical evidence | 5+ seconds large text alone |
| C14 | Environmental resolution | Show what remained changed | complete transformed place/system | final cause physically resolves into mark | cut to empty logo screen |

An episode should feature **≥6 distinct families** if length permits, but this is a planning heuristic, not an arbitrary visual quota. Any composition can be repeated if physical continuity adds explanatory value; repeated CAMERA/OBJECT/STATE combinations without new knowledge fail.

## 3. Subject-driven signature motion: never repeat the 'hero trick'
- **Samoa:** sheet of paper/calendar flips from Dec 29 to Dec 31 while crossed-out 30 shrinks; world-date-line boundary slides beneath island. Mechanism: time-zone boundary governance, not magical time reversal.
- **Lake Peigneur:** under-lake mine galleries flood via cross-section, shoreline ground deformation and rising surface vortex. Need properly staged water flow, exact geographic anchors. A 2D donut is not a lake.
- **Boston:** steel joint separates, pressure reduces and heavy viscous laminar/sheer bands shove period carts; source photo supplies factual aftermath. Don't reuse blue lake-ripple algorithm tinted brown.
- **Sweden:** measured lane change of authorized vehicles under street restriction; H sign and 04:50/05:00 timing controls. No every-car stampede.
- **Chicago:** jackscrew thread rotates and raises beam/building by incremental step; sewer grade is physically established first. Machinery must support the depicted mass.

## 4. Animation stage design example — Chicago 5.8-second lift sequence
**0.0–0.9s:** 1:1 physical screw-thread macro. An operator turns one handle ~90°; upper bearing moves a small, visible increment; ratchet sound matches each bite.
**0.9–1.8s:** camera reveals four adjacent supports and timber load-distributing beams; transparent arrows show weight into beds, NOT weight floating.
**1.8–2.9s:** camera rises to building brick bond, window jambs and storefront; paint dust shakes, sill shifts very slightly relative to old street line.
**2.9–4.0s:** historical 1860 Lake Street frontage widens; source tag '320 ft / 6,000 jacks'; three sections rise in plausible synchronized increments.
**4.0–5.8s:** street-to-foundation cutaway reveals brick being placed beneath; next scene derives its raised road surface from this aligned new grade.

**The source model must keep the building object constant across shots**; a flat PNG zoom followed by unrelated vector rectangles is an art-direction failure (Episode 007).

## 5. Map example — 006 Dagen H 4-second choreography
0–0.5s: actual neighborhood junction outlines from credible road plan; 0.5–1.5s: arrows show OLD left-hand movement; 1.5–2.4s: camera isolates a sign change and note 'traffic restricted'; 2.4–3.3s: 04:50 authorized move right; 3.3–4s: 05:00 travel resumes; if signs claim 06:00 general traffic, move that to subsequent timeline beat. Every moment teaches a distinct rule.

## 6. Editorial information choreography
- Every VO clause has an explicit object, action, and resultant state. Example: 'water entered underground galleries' = 1) shaft opening appears 2) flow propagates to gallery 3) crew route exits, not a subtitle over a still mine.
- **Single focal change rule:** darken/soften no-longer-relevant planes, enlarge active mechanism, return scene environment only after change is understood.
- **Precision:** facts such as 21 deaths, 52 miners escaping, 82.9% advisory vote, and 6,000 screws must be matched to appropriate subject/episode. Never repurpose a numeric template with wrong connotation or source.
- **Archival animation:** parallax extraction is allowed if genuine image boundaries can be separated credibly; do not create fabricated moving historical persons or actions.

## 7. Shot building and technical structure
Each scene is authored as deterministic `render(t, state)` with timeline-derived state, not accumulated real-time deltas. Scene assets are separated into world geometry, depth planes, mechanism, evidence, type and SFX triggers. Statically cache expensive high-resolution assets but render all type and geometry at native 4K. Composition states are keyed by **narration word events**, with scene layout reflowed when the voice changes. Include a debug layer visualizing caption zone, safe areas, asset native resolution, active stage and semantic motion event.

## 8. Six-shot visual diversity test
Print six **consecutive** major beats at phone size, remove captions and IDs, ask: which changed camera family, which new mechanism and which fact learned? FAIL if three-or-more shots could be mistaken for same still with updated words; FAIL if source art has a >2× detail-quality swing between adjacent shots. Record aspect/treatment counts in `shot_fingerprint.csv`.

## 9. Delivery QA
Motion grade passes only when (a) no placeholder humans/buildings where main focus, (b) captions do not obscure mechanism, (c) no >5-second narrated beat where meaningful picture change fails, (d) no sequence relies on an animated title floating in emptiness, (e) 3 detailed closeup samples from FIRST/MIDDLE/END meet same asset standard, and (f) shot-specific animation mechanism remains truthful.

# 08 — AUDIO, SOUND EFFECTS, SCORE & CENTER-CAPTION BIBLE
## Why the audio standard is no longer discretionary
At -37 dBFS / minimum 0.85s, Episode 004's *isolated natural voice* contains seven long silences: 4.62–8.16 (3.54s), 13.03–17.15 (4.11s), 25.82–30.20 (4.38s), 33.79–39.16 (5.37s), 47.65–51.16 (3.51s), 58.52–62.16 (3.64s) and 71.96–75.00 (3.04s). Episode 005 source logs independently show 2.86–5.12s gaps across seven preallocated scenes. Episodes 006 and 007: **zero** intervals exceeding 0.85s under the same threshold on available voice WAVs, with measured clip boundary gaps ~0.25–0.38s. These are reproducible technical findings, not subjective impressions. Detection depends on threshold and cannot replace critical listening.

## 1. Voice director's standard
- Natural, intelligible documentary storytelling in which astonishment is earned; not hyperactive, synthetic or breathless.
- Approve scripted pronunciation of place names and individuals, establish pronunciation reference voice, listen at normal speed.
- Intro opening word must be present by first visual action; first 3 seconds contain one clear complete idea.
- Record/assemble all spoken lines into ONE unpadded master sequence; trim leading/trailing quiet room from individual clips, preserve ~0.12–0.55s natural breath, never preassign empty scene slots.
- Any unexpected **>0.85s** speech silence editorial FAIL; any **>1.20s** narration gap between passages RELEASE FAIL, even with music; intentional >0.85s pauses need creative-director signoff and animated action that continues story (and may still fail release by user standard).
- Final word to video end **0.8–1.8s** normally. A 5-second voice-free static masthead is prohibited.
- Never rate-speed/time-stretch voices to solve a sync error. If VO overruns, rewrite/rerecord or extend visual mechanism meaningfully and adjust timeline.

## 2. Mechanical speech-gap QC
Decode voice stem to 48k WAV; use:

```bash
ffmpeg -i voice.wav -af 'silencedetect=noise=-37dB:d=0.85' -f null - 2>voice_silence.txt
```

Compute every gap from actual decoded signal and merge with per-clip measured true bounds. Also inspect thresholds −30dB/−42dB when room noise could mask speech offset; normal speech pauses can show lower-level breaths/noise so include listening. Export machine-readable `voice_gap_QA.json` with clip ID, spoken start/end, gap to next speech, scene ID, intended pause reason, max gap and final tail. Fail build if empty report, if a scene keeps a fixed overlong slot, or if any policy threshold exceeded.

## 3. Audio bus architecture
| Bus | Content | Audio-design standard | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| VOICE | approved narrator | up-front, natural, lightly de-essed; center stable | headphones + phone, every word |
| MUSIC | recorded/licensed evolving score | actual melody/harmony/harmonic rhythm and emotional arc, not buried sine pad | audible across dialogue gaps at phone volume |
| AMBIENCE | grounded location | scene-accurate street/water/earth/construction bed with spatial texture | spatial movement matches camera environment |
| DETAIL SFX | jack ratchet, ripples, drops, H sign clicks | clear transient with causal visible event | expected ±100–200ms trigger window |
| IMPACT | controlled rupture, collapse, steel fracture | intense but physically plausible and not over-compressed | no clipped bass or generic hit on every cut |
| TRANSITIONS | page, object, camera switch | optional, only when object moves/cut needs editorial signal | remove if transition is already clear |
| MASTER | sum with ducking and limiter | approximately -14 to -12 LUFS, ≤-1 dBTP, 48k stereo | measure actual delivered AAC/Opus |

Original score should use 3–4 clearly differentiated harmonic sections. Example Chicago: dampened felt piano + bowed strings → tentative click/rattle → steady ratchet-driven percussion at synchronized screw motif → wider harmonic arrival → reduced ending theme. Example Boston: ominous metal drones and documentary strings, pressure accent, sudden drop on consequence, restrained human-stakes coda. Avoid identical template soundtrack across all seven historical subjects.

## 4. Recorded effects vs synthesis
Inventory actual Curio Bureau Drive SFX and each delivered episode manifest before mix. Track `filename / owner / catalog URL / license / transformations / scene trigger / scene time / gain / pan`. Actual recorded effects are preferred for culturally and physically grounded sounds. A synthesized wave/ratchet is permitted only if it passes listening and provenance statements; do not falsely name it a field recording.

**Episode examples:** 004 reported recorded water/rumble/debris clips; check Freesound individual reuse license. 007 included original procedural Foley and a rumble source in source package; verify clip rights before publication. All sound must be inspected at comparable loudness to voice; a measurable stem is not necessarily audible or well-placed.

## 5. Caption identity and accessibility
- Exact font: Manrope 700/800; approximately 42–55px at logical 1080×1920, rendered vector-sharp at 2160×3840.
- Short, natural phrases one or two lines; no wide opaque dark banner across the lower third. Use transparent/low-visual-weight shaped support only where background readability demands.
- **Primary safe band:** vertical **42–58%** of visible picture; nominal y=1613–2227 native 4K. Caption midpoint variable based on scene, but always clear of app UI and main mechanism; recompose shot before moving caption into bottom UI.
- Horizontal default center; evidence panels and hero mechanics strategically above/below. On shots with centered machinery, create a designed inactive inset/negative-space slot for the phrase.
- Actual final spoken word boundaries must drive SRT and burned-in text. Edge TTS service sometimes emits zero word boundaries: **do not quietly estimate cues and declare final**. Use forced aligner (whisperX/MFA or other tested alignment) and manually check against recorded final voice.
- Regression suite overlays realistic TikTok right buttons/bottom profile and YouTube Shorts bottom info/right controls on representative **encoded MP4 and WebM frames**; FAIL on clipping, overlap, occlusion or unreadability at ~216px phone width.
- Image remains caption-clean when captions absent for alternate platform localization if practical. SRT delivered alongside burned captions.

## 6. Actual output verification
1. Export mix and voice WAV separately; measure the speech QA report first.
2. Master music/SFX on audio timeline anchored to narration; inspect SFX timing as visible object triggers.
3. Encode both H.264/AAC MP4 and VP9/Opus WebM at true native 4K.
4. Decode compressed audio from BOTH outputs and listen to random phrase joints + beginning/mid/end; use `ebur128`/`loudnorm` for measured report and true-peak metering.
5. Output alignment report includes caption onset error per phrase, video and audio duration difference, and a count of overlapping UI captions.
6. Require signed creative listening pass: compelling music, sound specificity, nonrobot voice, transitions with natural breath, and no cut-off ending.

# 09 — STORYTELLING & HOOK BIBLE
## Editorial signature: consequence → hidden system → human stakes → clear, earned payoff
Curio Bureau follows a mystery without abandoning evidence: show *the impossible-looking consequence*, then explain the structural or geographical system that made it possible. Each short needs **one central question** and an answer that changes how the viewer understands the first frame. Not every opening must involve danger; the surprise may be a missing date, an unbuilt foundation, a changed social rule or an astonishing scale.

## 1. Four proof-bearing story spines
- **Physical reversal:** expected flow of matter or objects contradicts intuition; explain underlying system. Lake Peigneur, Boston.
- **Rule reversal:** a policy or time convention visibly changes everyday life. Samoa, Dagen H.
- **Hidden infrastructure:** surprising physical feat is necessary because the original system has a flaw. Chicago.
- **Evidence puzzle:** an archival image/document contradicts a common belief; transform evidence into diagram rather than unverified reenactment. Suitable future episodes.

A new film selects one dominant spine; secondary spines are allowed if not muddying the question.

## 2. First five seconds as a working script
| Time | Story job | Image job | Voice job | Audio job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.7s | Surprise is already underway | recognizable action, single subject, high clarity | first phrase begins quickly | distinctive physical sound, not stock whoosh |
| 0.7–1.8s | Plain-language contrast | consequence becomes legible | short subject+verb, not preamble | support, clear diction |
| 1.8–3.0s | Provide minimum context | reveal unique object, location or scale | clarify impossible-looking claim | restrained accent |
| 3.0–5.0s | Create causal question | reveal hint of mechanism, withhold resolution | explicit/implicit 'How could that happen?' | introduce musical question motif |

**Disqualifiers:** intro logo, 'In the year...' prologue, blank frame, multiple simultaneous mysteries, precise claim unsupported by research, false reenactment, montage with no one readable event.

## 3. Six viewer-carrying turns for a ~75s film
- 0–5: why watch, physically.
- 5–14: where/when and what is unexpectedly at stake; maps or reality.
- 14–28: hidden system / contradiction.
- 28–45: key mechanism and turning point; show genuinely animated complex structure, not held slide.
- 45–60: humans, investigation, or second-order effect; bring documentary evidence into foreground.
- 60–75: earned reversal/outcome + brief final mark; often return to first visual element from a newly informed viewpoint.
These are **story beat estimates**, not an excuse to pad narration. The film must FOLLOW the real voice track; scenes may shorten/extend when there is enough new content.

## 4. Retention micro-promises
At each act transition write two columns: `What the viewer currently thinks` → `What the next visual changes`. If the new scene does not change knowledge, remove or combine it. Avoid continuous question-slinging; the audience needs partial answers and escalating stakes. A reveal is not merely a label flying in.

## 5. Episode-specific examples / improvements
- **001:** cold open with 30th of month disappearing; promise 'can a country skip a calendar date?' After explanation of International Date Line, show human scheduling consequence; no claim time itself vanished.
- **004:** shoreline collapse then mine; don't falsely portray full documented footage. At 35s human evacuation shows real consequence. 52 miners all escaped is accurate as historical source; never infer identities.
- **005:** brown wave hits street before identifying molasses; the hidden answer is weak riveted infrastructure and ignored reports, not 'syrup is funny'. 21 deaths demand sober transition.
- **006:** driver/road switch at 04:50 under traffic restriction; provide advisory 1955 vote, 1963 law, 05:00 rule, 06:00 staged traffic without implying all drivers crossed at once.
- **007:** actual screw handle moves, whole ornate storefront rises slightly; why? sewer and higher street grade; no entire city rose simultaneously. Compare 1860 Lake Street and 1861 Tremont House separately.

## 6. Hook workshop template (three variants before production)
For each of A consequence / B mechanism / C human-or-data contradiction:
`FACT-LOCK / FRAME0 OBJECT / CAMERA POSITION / VISIBLE MOTION <0.5s / FIRST 13 WORDS / 2.5s CONTEXT / 5s OPEN QUESTION / SOUND ACCENT / COPYRIGHT STATUS / MUTE COMPREHENSION / FAILURE RISK`.
Three alternative hooks should look materially different—not three headlines over the same fixed frame. Choose after 3–5 independent phone-size mute reviewers can identify the physical action.

## 7. What not to infer from social-video public counts
High views can reflect creator reach, topic, packaging, paid distribution or algorithm conditions. Views are **not** measured retention. Private YouTube/TikTok analytics are required to answer which exact 1–5s frames make viewers stay; use hypotheses, A/B-ish tests and coded post-mortems until those metrics become accessible.

## 8. Outro
No generic 'subscribe' before factual resolution. Last spoken clause should answer the first question while revealing a larger system. Integrate 'Curio Bureau' for a short 0.8–1.8s branded tail on a still-moving appropriate environment; make rewatch loop ethical, not confusing. Sound must conclude just after the final word, never 4–5s later.

# 10 — CURIO BUREAU PRODUCTION WORKFLOW V2
**Primary operational change:** decisions are approved before high-cost full 4K renders; final *voice* is the master timeline, while every visual beat receives a chosen composition and validated asset-quality target.

## New project structure
```
curio/episodes/008_slug/
  00_brief/{story_angle,claim_lock,research_sources,rights}.md
  01_hooks/{A,B,C}/(first_frame.png,hook_animatic.mp4,hook_eval.md)

  05_animation/{studio/,scene_manifest.json,render_tests/}
  06_audio/{voice/music/ambience/foley/effects/mix.wav,cue_sheet.csv}
  07_captions/{aligned_words.json,final.srt,ui_mask_previews/}
  08_render/{frames_4k_png/,master_4k.mp4,master_4k.webm}
  09_qa/{technical.json,art_review.md,sound_listen.md,source_audit.md,contact_sheets/}
```
Each scene manifest entry: `id, word_start, word_end, story_claim, source_ids, shot_family, hero_asset_ids, camera, layer_depths, state_changes, source_footnote, caption_free_region, SFX_ids, transition_type, QA_status`. Master audio word starts/ends are final timing authority.

## Stage 0 — Evidence retrieval and reality check
Locate all previous art references and per-episode source materials. No fabricated access. Inventories distinguish `verified local`, `library unmounted`, `external linked`, `not available`. Audit any reused SFX licenses before copying.

## Stage 1 — Historical research lock (P0 gate)
Every factual statement: source link, exact supporting phrase, event date, publication date, conflict/uncertainty, original image-rights status. If claim contested, script uses 'historians estimate' or it is removed. No spectacular animation contradicts event mechanics. Deliver claim_lock.csv/research.md. **Gate:** independent source check for numbers, human cost, geography, quoted lines.

## Stage 2 — Hook board and first-frame proof (P0/P1 gate)
Write 3 materially different hooks. Draw 3 actual phone-scale FIRST FRAME images and animate 0–5s versions with scratch voice. Evaluator must identify the anomalous physical action by ~1s and topic by ~3s. Decide angle before script. **Gate:** no blank logo, unrecognizable action or illegible center.

## Stage 3 — Narration recording FIRST (P0 gate)
Approve 65–85s human-paced script where actual duration is story-dependent. Record voice via approved narrator and save isolated WAV. Trim genuine leading/trailing quiet. Report all per-clip starts/ends, gaps, intra-clip silence, tail and pronunciation. If any >0.85s unintended pause or >1.2s hard-release gap, **ripple-edit**, rerecord or rewrite NOW. Do not fill scene slots with silence. Obtain verified word boundaries via forced alignment/manual test. **Gate:** voice + SRT approved.

## Stage 4 — Shot choreography and asset scope (P1 gate)
Create detailed beat sheet and 12–25 major shot descriptions, EACH with own composition family, physical system, camera, asset list, annotation and sound trigger. Print a shot family strip. No >2 consecutive same framing family unless progressive mechanism demonstrably changes. Lock subject-owned hero motion and minimum asset complexity. **Gate:** five style frames (opening, first abstract explanation, first real historic environment, most difficult mechanism, final insight) meet one art standard.

## Stage 5 — Low-cost animation animatic (P1 gate)
At 540×960/1080×1920 previews, sync actual narration, rough music/SFX. Every 2–3s there must be meaningfully new information or a consciously paced factual absorption beat. Review complete 0–5s, all act joins, largest graphic changes, muted phone test. **Gate:** editor/art director approve whole 75s storyline BEFORE 4K.

## Stage 6 — Asset building and evidentiary rights
Draw objects to exact hero quality: five major depth planes, recognizable architecture/machinery, source-aware geometry, original textures, max native raster res demands. Match palette and typography lock. Maintain source table per photo/sound; if license uncertain, swap to labeled original graphic. **Gate:** weakest 20% of shots are still credible as finished editorial illustrations.

## Stage 7 — Motion & camera engineering
Use deterministic `render(t)`, scene manifest state and single audio timeline. Separate camera, physical event, labels, safe captions and SFX triggers. Add instrumented snapshot mode `render(scene, t, debug=true)` and frame overlays showing boxes/shot families. Test full source on mobile preview and random-scrub consistency. **Gate:** no glitch/dynamic discontinuity; no unexpected static holds; no same asset repeating as entire environment.

## Stage 8 — Professional audio and subtitles (P0/P1 gate)
Compose/clear music with real development; use recorded/provenance tracked Foley where appropriate; VOICE/MUSIC/AMB/DETAIL/IMPACT stems; critical listening on headphones AND phone. Re-run measured voice gaps; align captions to approved final audio; check TikTok/Shorts overlays. Music should be audible *as music*, not just as spectrum energy. **Gate:** listeners pass intelligibility and specificity.

## Stage 9 — PRE-4K QC (mandatory cost saver)
Render 1080×1920 30fps proof with **final** music/voice/captions. Review entire video, scene joins, headings, 3 shot-family strips, intro/outro and source citations. Any P0 or P1 flaw sends film BACK to edit. Never begin 2,300-frame 4K encode without reviewer signoff.

## Stage 10 — Real native 4K render
Source HTML/SVG/Canvas renders lossless native 2160×3840 PNGs at exact frame N/30s. Do not resize 1080p video to 4K. Mix stereo 48kHz/24bit WAV; H.264 MP4 CRF~15 AAC ~320k + VP9/Opus WebM. Preserve exact typography and center captions in both.

## Stage 11 — Encoded-film QA (P0)
ffprobe verifies both output streams independently. Decode first/last, random midpoints, transitions and 100%-size pixel crops. Verify duration, FPS, no missing frames/truncation, fonts, source-asset effective resolution, visually readable captions with real UI masks, -14 to -12 LUFS and true-peak ≤-1 dBTP, no voice gaps, no buried SFX, montage rhythm, all claim citations. **Gate:** QA PASSED only when both technical and subjective creative criteria are green.

## Stage 12 — Publish learning loop
Save final promo/thumbnail, performance analytics at day 1/7/30, hypothesis vs observed retention, shot fingerprint and creative retrospective. Record which hook/visual explanation genuinely worked. Update Bible via changelog and version bump; don't silently overwrite inherited standards.

## Expensive mistake prevention
The previous production habit rendered 4K before correcting primitive artwork and caption issues. V2 requires **three explicit approvals** before final encode: (a) full storyboard asset quality, (b) full 1080p animatic playback with correct speech gaps, and (c) mobile caption/UI test. Rendering success cannot upgrade 'QA FAILED' to 'FINAL'.

# 12 — ANTI-REPETITION SYSTEM: HOW TO BE CONSISTENT WITHOUT LOOKING IDENTICAL
## Problem from observed episodes
005 reuses the same pale building grid and brown sinusoidal flood across ~37–46s; 006 repeats the same road/facades/H-sign backdrop across ~37–59s; 007 repeatedly falls back to its opening illustration after midsection diagrams. The brand is identifiable, but animation novelty collapses. Consistency belongs in **craft standards and evidence language**, not in repeated scenery or composition template.

## 1. Shot fingerprint ledger
Every shot logs:
`episode / start / end / factual claim / primary family C01–C14 / hero object / camera axis / camera motion / environment ID / background asset ID / transition ID / annotation type / evidence medium / color mode / sound motif / semantic state change / QA status`.

**Near-duplicate rule:** Flag adjacent shots if ≥4 of these five match: (1) shot family, (2) camera scale/axis, (3) main environment, (4) main object orientation, (5) annotation positioning; and no visible new causal state develops. Two consecutive may be allowed for a mechanism; three require documented justification and side-by-side director review.

**Cross-episode duplicate rule:** A signature hero object, transition sequence, or environment asset from a previous film may be reused only for cross-reference with a justified source or relevant geographic continuity; otherwise design anew. E.g., do not repurpose Chicago's abstract ratchet waves as Boston molasses and assume color changes count as novelty.

## 2. Visual Variety Index (VVI), diagnostic not an optimization game
For an episode with N major shots:
- `F = distinct major shot families / max(1, min(N,8))`, capped at 1.
- `C = distinct camera systems / max(1,min(N,5))`, capped at 1. System means fixed photo-macro / macro object / ground level / overhead cartography / cross-section, not tiny pan differences.
- `M = unique subject-owned physical-mechanism actions / max(1,min(N,4))`, capped at 1.
- `D = 1 − (near_duplicate_adjacent_pairs / max(1,N−1))`.
- `E = share of important claims directly visualized with source evidence or true explanation, not only title typography`.
**VVI = 0.20F + 0.15C + 0.25M + 0.20D + 0.20E.** Range 0–1. This is a review cue, not a substitute for creative judgment; a calm emotional hold may be exactly right. Score should prompt conversation, not enforce frantic animation.

## 3. Quality continuity floor
Art complexity is measured at five fixed checkpoints: frame0, 25%, hardest mechanism, 75%, final. Reviewer scores each 0–5 for detailed silhouette, materials/texture, perspective, spatial credibility, and story-specific cues. **No main-shot composite should drop more than 1 point from the opening reference in the same category** unless intentionally minimalist and dramatically justified. Episode 007 fails this qualitative criterion (poster-grade opening / flat mechanical schematics).

## 4. Scene adjacency exercises
Take every six consecutive major shot frames (captions removed). View side by side at 180px width. If they look like a generic web slideshow or if 4+ are the same dark field with a changing heading, rearrange and redesign.

Example re-edit for Sweden 33–55s:
1. External empty street soundscape / low POV under restriction (C04/C01)
2. Ultra-macro mechanical H sign reveal (C03)
3. Clock at 04:50 overlaying a real junction map (C06/C12)
4. Top-down authorized vehicles moving right (C09)
5. Ground-level right-hand traffic resumes at 05:00 (C01)
6. Worker reveals new road marking / archive photo angle (C05)
Current cut reused essentially the same illustrated street and cars as backgrounds. The six-step redesign can keep map orientation consistent while camera, focal scale, material and state progress distinctly.

Example re-edit for Chicago 12–55s:
1. Archival sewer-plan macro (C08)
2. Brick arched sewer section with grade/soil gradient (C02)
3. Exterior shop doorway stranded below new pavement (C09)
4. Macro steel jackscrew bearing rotates under beam (C03)
5. Ground perspective whole storefront frontage rises (C01)
6. Lit interior with shop continuing to operate (C04)
7. Contemporary 1860 lithograph details and source date (C05)
None needs a generic flat title card. All maintain a recognizable source-annotated visual grammar.

## 5. Project-level restrictions
- Never put one full-frame generated image into **three or more separate major acts** to conceal incomplete motion. Separate/deepen/mask/map if reusing a scene as continuity, or create new licensed/source geometry.
- At least **one custom explanatory hero object** must be modeled/drawn at high detail per episode, then used in 2–4 transformed states; this maintains subject specificity.
- At least **one verified archival evidence object** (where historically available and rights-cleared) must influence an animated explanation; if no usable archive, log it and use clearly labeled original reconstruction.
- Each major act should usually change either camera axis, physical environment or evidence medium; do not change all three at once every time or viewer will lose geography.
- No base 'generic map' object without verified geographic data. No more than one repeating generic whoosh. No ambiguous engineered load paths.
- A shared intro/outro is a semantic signal, but logo appears only after story is underway and last 0.8–1.8s.

## 6. Approval protocol
Art director annotates shot ledger `distinct / justified-repeat / harmful-repeat`. A harmful-repeat mark is a P1 remediation task. QA automator can flag exact IDs/treatment equality; a human approves whether causality actually developed. Preserve historical accuracy over artificial variety; never choose a false camera/viewpoint merely to increase VVI.
