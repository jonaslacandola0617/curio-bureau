# Reference mastery — Curio Bureau editorial film grammar

## Verified reference families
1. Vox's **Borders**: geographic stories made legible through sequential map highlighting, location-based documentary footage, political boundaries, and deepening regional context. Series descriptions and examples: https://www.thehumanimprint.com/?p=2572
2. Vox's **Missing Chapter**: archival animation, original reporting, historically grounded documents; Vox Media's own description: https://www.voxmedia.com/2021/5/10/22428582/voxs-missing-chapter-returns-for-season-two/
3. Contemporaneous Samoa cartographic reporting (BBC/ABC) as FACTUAL map guidance, **not borrowed visual assets**.

This is an editorial production grammar derived from those references and professional motion techniques, **not a claim of per-frame breakdown** for uninspected proprietary source videos.

## Curio Bureau motion system
- **Visual subject continuity:** a date square punches into a map-grid cell; map longitude line becomes timeline; timeline transforms into the statutory article rule; section 8 highlight yields a payroll line; printed underline becomes the Curio Bureau logo stroke.
- **Three visual depths:** background paper/maritime grain; middle atlas/map/document; foreground live tracking marks and annotations. Avoid flat single-plane layouts.
- **Camera language:** 1.03–1.18 push or restrained 80–140px pan, 0.7–2.2 seconds, hold clear before a claim; captions remain screen-anchored.
- **Transformation:** use shared geometry and anchored masks, not page-to-blank fades.
- **Typographic architecture:** Playfair Display dramatic headings (48–104px typical); Manrope labels/data/captions; every large phrase fit-tested in final pixels.
- **Editorial effects:** restrained multiply/screen halftone, off-white archival paper, narrow copper-rule annotations, subtle image/depth displacement; no generic website cards.
- **Contrast rhythm:** visually dense documentary proof alternates with 1–2-second clean conceptual reveal; screens are never empty for narration.
- **Easing:** scene build smoothstep/quintic; collision or documentary mark sharp 100–250ms ease-out; moving map labels ease in phase after location focus.
- **Sound:** cue whose real envelope matches object motion. SFX at every principal change with a distinct class, not indiscriminate whoosh.
- **Composition:** 84px minimum horizontal safe; title safe within x=100..980, y=130..1300; subtitles at 1450–1650; bottom UI-free > 1680.

## Frame-study procedure for future films
Collect three frames around each decisive cut (before/mid/after); annotate narration claim, moving graphic, camera anchor, cut geometry, photographic layers, graphic data, typography hierarchy, SFX envelope, and evidence source. Do not invent frame descriptions when source video inaccessible.

## Failure diagnosis for old cut
- fake geography created by arbitrary polygons → Natural Earth projection.
- giant uncapped canvas text → measured Playfair fitting and bounding-box QA.
- quiet raw oscillators → deliberate audible music bed with offline audio mixing.
- SFX reused softly/unmetrically → per-event Drive library cue sheet.
- only scene-boundary cuts → animate information within each scene.
- captions absent → word-boundary-based burnt captions + SRT.
- expensive realtime capture then low-bitrate transcode → deterministic frames and CRF16–17 MP4.
