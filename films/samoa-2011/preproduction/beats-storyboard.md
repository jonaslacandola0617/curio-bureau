## Storyboard / animatic acceptance criteria
- No scene uses large unwrapped text at fixed x or y outside frame.
- Evidence visually identifies the primary-source Act as a re-typeset representation, not fabricated newspaper.
- History + geography + policy cohere as one graphic continuity.
- Captions must not cross-map labels or text.
- Each major visual transition has a sonic event or intentional dramatic silence.
- No frame may be a generic centered slogan without moving evidence or concrete narrative image.
