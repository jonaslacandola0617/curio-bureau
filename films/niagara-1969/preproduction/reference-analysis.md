**Editorial filmmaking translation**
1. Act-one sensory reversal: start with *extremely dense live-feeling waterfall motion*, then the water volume decreases as an upstream engineering diagram arrives.
2. Match graphic geometry: waterfall flow ribbon shrinks to single chart stroke; chart stroke becomes the river channel and temporary rock dam plan-view.
3. Master vertical editorial zones: top title 100–580; visual evidence 620–1390; burnt-in Manrope captions 1450–1630; small folio 1750+.
4. Camera: restrained shift and crop pan on original 2.5D footage; vector map reveals geodetic anchors, then turns into explicit schematic layout instead of fake map.
5. Archival collage: manila newspaper backboard, factual 1969/1975 document folios, high-contrast historical photo with real rights attribution.
6. Time-based animated diagram: barrier stones fill river channel and flow arrows bend toward Horseshoe rather than pure decorative label motion.
7. Geology scene: sediment layers, cracks, 48 core hole dots, piezometer needles; no oversimplified empty rectangles for actual rock.
8. Return: reverse flow diagram to waterfall, full waterfall roar and music return; avoid instant mass restart.
9. Final: large Playfair Display policy insight, citation and masthead; make end card hold 1.5–2.5s, visually inspect 63.96s.

**Animation rules:** Absolute renderAt(frame/30), controlled 0.8–2.2s ease, purposeful motion cue for each large change. One evidence reveal per sentence. Visual transformation > slideshow fade. Fully composed each frame, no clipped titles.
