## Historical fact lock
- US Army 23rd Headquarters Special Troops activated January 20, 1944; unit of roughly 1,100 troops. Sources US Army, WWII Museum.
- Unit used inflatable rubber decoys such as tanks, trucks and artillery; spoof radio traffic; recordings/loudspeakers of troop and armored-vehicle movements; and visible decoy tracks and visual theatre. Source museum 2024 Canvas and Camouflage, Army 2021.
- 22 deception operations during last year in Europe. Museum 2020/2024.
- In 1945 March Operation Viersen near Rhine, Ghost Army impersonated two divisions / up to approximately 30,000 men using inflatable equipment; actual Ninth Army divisions crossed with limited resistance in reported museum account. Source museum 2020.
- The 3132nd Signal Service Company used 500-pound speakers on halftracks; sounds up to 15 miles under favorable circumstances. This particular number comes from museum and can be omitted from VO if timing.
- The historical unit's exact drawing props/positions are NOT documented by our recreated illustration. All film scenes MUST indicate original **ILLUSTRATIVE RECONSTRUCTION**, not false documentary frames.
- No re-use of Vox tutorial footage, museum paintings/photos, film music, copyrighted military recordings, real soldiers' voices. The audio is synthetic original score and effects, NOT genuine 1945 field recordings.
- Human stakes should be described carefully: "helped conceal the crossing" rather than assert quantified lives saved or particular enemy troop dispositions where unsupported.
- Correct ambiguous claim: "30,000" is the *simulated force* of two divisions, **not** 30,000 actual soldiers hidden in one field. "1,100" is entire specialist deception force, not an exact participant count at all individual moments.
