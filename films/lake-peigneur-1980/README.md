### Story
On November 20, 1980, a salt mine beneath Lake Peigneur in Louisiana flooded as lake water entered the mine workings during nearby oil-drilling operations. All 52 people underground escaped (MSHA FY1981 institutional account). The lake drained rapidly, a huge whirlpool engulfed equipment and shoreline, Delcambre Canal reversed course, and water replenished the basin. The precise initiating cause and responsibility remain disputed.
