## Hard regressions forbidden
- Repeated 3–5 second silent narration holes: normal gap 0.12–0.55s; unplanned >0.85s editorial fail; >1.20s release fail; check inside voice clips, not just between them.
- Subtitles behind platform controls: place Manrope phrases around vertical center (42–58%), compose shots around them, and test TikTok/Shorts UI overlay.
- Fake 4K: actual 2160×3840 30fps BOTH primary H.264/AAC MP4 and VP9/Opus WebM, separate validation, no upscaled 1080p.
- Inconsistent art: no generic card/rectangle/stickfigure substitute for scene-specific fully designed 2D/2.5D mechanism; premium shot-detail floor across whole film, no reused layout choreography.
- Buried score/SFX, robotic voice, unlicensed or falsely presented archival content, estimated captions labeled word-aligned, unsupported historical claims.
